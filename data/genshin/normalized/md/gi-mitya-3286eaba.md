Mitya ( Russian : Митя Mitya ) is an upcoming playable Electro character in Genshin Impact .

Mitya is a scientist from the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya and an expert in the field of energy research. Brilliant yet extremely sociable and outgoing, he has a tendency to dodge work and occasionally finds himself offset by his own sentient scarf whenever he goes overboard with his conversations.


## Official Introduction

Genius At Play

"Research differs from production — efficiency is not the gold standard. I have worked alongside many gifted individuals, and I know well that talent and eccentricity tend to go hand in hand. But the one thing that never disappoints is the 'answers' they provide."

— Pierro , "The Jester," responding to the insinuation that Mitya holds special privileges

"I've applied for his scarf to be granted greater authority to help prevent leaks of classified information. In the vast majority of security inquiries, bystanders are considered innocent."

— Valeriy , Commander, Special Assignments Battalion

Experts tend to be remarkably efficient, capable of producing astonishing results in very little time. Yet for them, "attendance requirements" designed around the pace of the average researcher amount to a cage.

Whiling away the hours would be easy enough. But the world has so much more to offer, and wouldn't it be a shame to miss out on all that color?

And so, the energy expert who ought to be buried in important work can often be found wandering the streets of Snezhnograd , enjoying the leisure his talent has bought him.

Sometimes he catches the Korolevskiy Troupe 's latest production, and sometimes he shares a table with a stranger at Podvorye Restaurant , and sometimes, on a fine sunny day, he gives his scarf, that dear companion of his, a wash and hangs it out to dry...

People expect a researcher to devote himself to his work, or at the very least, not neglect his duties. But he would argue: how can someone who takes no joy in life hope to do good work?

To borrow a seafaring analogy, meticulous reasoning is like drawing an accurate sea chart, while a flash of inspiration is when a new continent suddenly comes into view through a spyglass. Clearly, the latter is the greater achievement, and a far more exhilarating one.

And so he sets out everything he finds "unreasonable" in letters, proposals, and leave requests, appends them to the last page of his research reports, and submits the lot in the hope of catching his superiors' attention.

Eventually, he got his wish. His increasingly cavalier approach to his schedule, coupled with his habit of taking it upon himself to rewrite absence reports as further arguments in his own defense, earned him the assistance of a major who took time management very seriously indeed. Congratulations were in order.
