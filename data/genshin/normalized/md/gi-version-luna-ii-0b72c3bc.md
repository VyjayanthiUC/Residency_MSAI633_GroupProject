## Adjustments & Optimizations

Enemies:

- Optimizes the combat experience in the " Knuckle Duckle " challenge. The summoned "Stamping Devices" will descend faster when positioned too high.
- Optimizes the targeting experience of character skills when challenging the enemies "Local Legend: Crab Tsar " and "Battle-Scarred Rock Crab."

Audio:

- Candace 's English VA will be updated. Historical content will be gradually updated in subsequent versions.
- Supplements some English voice-over resources for Layla , Lynette , and Tighnari in " Travelers' Tales ."
- Supplements some English voice-over resources for Lynette in "About Escoffier."
- Supplements some English voice-over resources for Tighnari in Version 5.5's Tribal Chronicles quest " Field Fungal Traces ."
- Supplements some English voice-over resources for Arlecchino in Genius Invokation TCG .
- Adjusts the Japanese voice lines for Lauma in "Joining Party: III."
- Adjusts certain Japanese voice lines for Dori in "Travelers' Tales" and the Archon Quest " A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves ."

System:

- Adds the entrance to " Miliastra Wonderland " and related quests to " Stellar Reunion "; complete all "Miliastra Wonderland"-related quests to obtain Geodes of Replication ×480.
- After the Version update, the tutorial button will be permanently displayed on the right side of the mini- map on mobile devices. (Before the update, mobile players had to navigate through relatively complicated steps: "Paimon Menu" - "Archive" - "Tutorials" to access previously read tutorials.)
- Increases the Friends cap from 100 to 200.

Serenitea Pot:

When redeeming items in the Realm Depot , the quantity is automatically set to the maximum amount available.

Genius Invokation TCG:

Optimizes the text descriptions for certain card effects.

Other:

- The Luna Treasure Compass at exploration ability Lv. 1 and Lv. 2 displays treasures and treasure-related clues within a range increased by 100% after the update.
- Streamlines the overall dialogue flow for the "Any tips on finding ore in ...?" option at Blacksmith across all regions, optimizes interaction and text prompts while tracking Rich Ore Reserves.

Genius Invokation TCG Balance Adjustment:

- Adjusts the activation mechanism of the "Discard" effect. Players can no longer preview the outcome after related "Discard" effects are triggered. Affected cards include: "Bonecruncher's Energy Block," "Qucusaurus," "Portable Power Saw," "Prospector's Drill," "The Dockhand's Assistant," "Conductor's Top Hat," "Flying Squad, Attack!," Chasca's Event Card "Shadowhunt Shell," and the skill of Character Cards "Lord of Eroded Primal Fire" and "Xinyan."
- Adjusts the effect of Chasca 's Event Card "Shadowhunt Shell": The trigger condition has been changed from "when this card is played or you Discard it" to "when this card is played or you Discard it from your Hand."
- Adjusts the activation mechanism of Food Event Cards with healing effects: They can now target Character Cards that are already at full health.
- Adjusts the effect of the Combat Status "Deep Devourer's Domain" of the Character Card " All-Devouring Narwhal ": The effect has been changed from "cards you have Discarded or used for Tune will be consumed" to "cards you have Discarded or used for Tune from your Hand will be consumed."
- Adjusts the effect of "Dark Shadow" summoned by Character Card "All-Devouring Narwhal": The effect that was previously triggered during "End Phase and when you declare the end of your Round", will now only be triggered during "End Phase."

## Known Issues

- There is an issue whereby some Travelers who play Miliastra Wonderland on PC are encountering a problem whereby their "Character Skill 4" and "Craftsperson Keymap 12" are both set to the same key, Z. Travelers who encounter this issue can go to " Settings > Controls ," reset the key mappings to default, and then reconfigure them as needed to resolve the problem.
- There is an issue whereby two Manekins owned by the same Traveler can be added to the party at the same time under certain circumstances. This issue will be fixed in a future version.
- There is an issue whereby some sound effects are missing (including sound effects for certain skills of the characters Ganyu , Keqing , and Freminet ). This issue will be fixed in a future version.
- There is an issue whereby there is a small possibility of missing combat-related audio (such as character, enemy, or object sounds) on some mobile devices under high load conditions. Since this issue is related to device performance stability, optimizations will be made in Version "Luna III" after stability testing.
- There is an issue whereby when the enemy " Frostnight Herra " is in the "Gloomveiled" state, some Character Talent effects that cannot deal damage will abnormally accumulate "damage taken progress" during the "Gloomveiled" period. This issue will be fixed in a future version.
- There is an issue whereby when equipping headwear for a Manekina with the " Candlelit Wizard: Pumpkin Candy " cosmetic, the bun hairstyle displays an abnormal hair color if "Hide Back of Hair" is selected in the component adjustment settings. This issue will be fixed in a future version.
