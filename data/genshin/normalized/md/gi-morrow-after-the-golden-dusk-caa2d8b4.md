## Glossary

Decoding: Fagio will switch to this mode when using a Charged Attack. When in this mode, Fagio will engage any opponents ahead with a sweeping fire attack, as well as fire condensed beams at intervals, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . Radiance: Stellar-Conduct : The condensed beams will instead deal AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG.
Decoding Power: When in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power will continuously increase. When its Decoding Power reaches the maximum, Fagio will be unable to maintain or re-enter Decoding mode.
