# Musecatcher

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Musecatcher | type=talent | effective=2025-09-10 -->


## Infobox

Name: Musecatcher
Character: Aino
Talent Type: Elemental Skill
Category: Combat
Description: Aino throws a Musecatcher forward, dealing Hydro DMG and dragging her along behind it. When the dragging stops, Aino deals AoE Hydro DMG to nearby opponents. Holding the Skill will cause it to behave differently. Hold Enter Aiming Mode to adjust the Musecatcher's throwing direction.
scale_att1: Scaling ATK
Cooldown: 10s

"Dreams — and snacks — should be seized with your own hands... Kerplunk-a-tron counts as my hand, right?"

Musecatcher is Aino 's Elemental Skill .


## Gameplay Notes

- When this skill hits at least one enemy , it generates 3 Elemental Particles .

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 1, The Theory of Ash—Field Equilibrium : "After Aino uses her Elemental Skill Musecatcher or her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler , her Elemental Mastery will be increased by 80. The Elemental Mastery of other nearby active party members will be increased by 80 for 15s. The Elemental Mastery-increasing effects of this Constellation do not stack."
- Constellation Level 4, Butter and Cats and the Law of Energy Supply : "When the Elemental Skill Musecatcher hits an opponent, it will restore 10 Elemental Energy for Aino. Energy can be restored to her in this manner once every 10s."
- Constellation Level 5, Perpetual Turbine of Metal and Light : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
