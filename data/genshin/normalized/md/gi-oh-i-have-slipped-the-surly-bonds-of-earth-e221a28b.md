"Oh! I Have Slipped the Surly Bonds of Earth" is Odette 's Level 5 Constellation .


## Trivia

- The name of her Level 5 Constellation is a reference to the opening line of the sonnet High Flight by World War II fighter pilot and war-poet John Gillespie Magee Jr.
