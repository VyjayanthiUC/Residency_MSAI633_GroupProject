## Infobox

Name: Stellar-Conduct
Type: Elemental Reactions
Group: Stellar Glimmer Reactions

Stellar-Conduct is the Stellar Glimmer Reaction triggered by inflicting Cryo on a target that is already affected by Electro or vice versa, replacing Superconduct while a character with a corresponding Stellar Jubilee Passive is in the party . After triggering a Stellar-Conduct reaction, a Starlight Prism will be created, briefly transforming the surrounding area into a Polestar Field. The Polestar Field increases the base reaction coefficient of the Stellar-Conduct reaction, grants Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus to the party, and reduces the Physical RES of all enemies. Hitting enemies within the field with Cryo or Electro attacks will store the energy and grant buffs to the party.


## Starlight Prism

After Stellar-Conduct is triggered, the resulting Starlight Prism lasts for 7s and will continuously sustain a Polestar Field around it. Inside the field, hits from Cryo and Electro attacks on enemies that apply an element (as limited by Internal Cooldown ) are recorded by the Prism. The initial hit that created the Prism is not included. Every 4s, the count is reset, and buffs lasting 4s are granted based on the number of hits recorded:

- If at least 1 Cryo or Electro hit is recorded, the Base Reaction Coefficient for Stellar-Conduct (which starts at 1) is increased by 40% plus an additional 5% for each recorded hit, up to a maximum of 12 hits.
- Characters inside the field have their Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus increased by 20%. If at least 1 Cryo or Electro hit is included, Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus is instead increased by 28% plus an additional 1% for each recorded hit, up to a maximum of 12 hits.
- All enemies in the field have their Physical RES reduced by 40%.
- Characters inside the field will not lose any heat or Kresnik Energy in the Snezhnayan open world. Additionally, if the character's Kresnik Energy is below 30%, it will immediately be restored to 30%.
- Prime Ice Constructs and firearms will receive certain buffs while inside a Polestar Field (see Prime Ice Construct Archive and individual firearm pages).

In the Snezhnayan open world, if a Starlight Prism exists while the player is not in combat, it will turn into a Prism Mirage after its duration expires, which the player can interact with to reactivate it. Reactivated Starlight Prisms last indefinitely and have the same properties as Prime Ice Constructs , meaning they have placement limits, can appear as co-op constructs , and can receive likes. Reactivated Starlight Prisms can be removed by interacting with them or via the "Manage Prime Ice Constructs" feature on the map .

## Damage

Main article: Damage § Stellar Glimmer Reaction Damage

Stellar-Conduct does no DMG on its own. The only way to deal Stellar-Conduct DMG is through character Talents and Constellations. The DMG dealt can be either Cryo or Electro , depending on the Talent or Constellation that dealt it.


## Tutorial

Elemental Reaction: Stellar-Conduct:
- When Cryo comes in contact with Electro , a special Stellar-Conduct reaction will be triggered if certain conditions are also met. When this happens, a Starlight Prism is generated, and the surrounding area turns into a wondrous Polestar Field for a short period of time. All characters inside the Polestar Field receive a Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus, and when opponents within the field are hit by Cryo or Electro attacks, the field will store the dissipating elemental energies and then completely release them every 4s. This further increases the aforementioned DMG Bonus and also increases the Base Stellar-Conduct DMG coefficient for 4s. Additionally, opponents within the Polestar Field will also have their Physical RES lowered. Certain characters will also gain the effects of Radiance: Stellar-Conduct when within the Polestar Field, enhancing certain Talents and skill effects. Certain characters can deal Stellar-Conduct DMG to opponents using their Talents, and this DMG can trigger CRIT Hits.

Starlight Prism:
- In certain places in Snezhnaya , a Starlight Prism will also generate a Prism Mirage that lasts for a short time at the same location when it vanishes. Approach the mirage to activate the Starlight Prism again, at which point it will remain on the field permanently. Prisms activated by such interactions have a chance to appear in other Travelers' worlds. Similarly, Prisms from other Travelers' worlds may also appear in your world. In certain places in Snezhnaya, the Polestar Field also presents with additional capabilities: · When inside a Polestar Field, the Eye of Graeae will not lose any energy. If the Eye's energy level is below 30%, it will also be immediately restored to 30%. · It also appears that Prime Ice Constructs inside a Polestar Field will gain various wondrous buffs...

"Stellar-Conduct" and Firearms...:
- When inside a Polestar Field created by the Stellar-Conduct reaction, you will also get additional buffs with certain firearms.

## Glossary

Polestar Field: When Stellar-Conduct is triggered, the surrounding area will transform into a Polestar Field for a short period of time, and when inside a Polestar Field, certain characters will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state, which grants various skill buffs.
Solo Dance Double: A psychic double of Odette's that dances a duet with her. May be summoned to the field through her Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst. Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves to periodically attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . A maximum of one Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette is allowed on the field at any one time. Additionally, when there is a Dance Double summoned by Odette nearby, her Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers is changed to the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling.
Marvelous Splendor: After unlocking the Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One," Odette will obtain a special effect when she summons her Solo Dance Double. Every stack of Marvelous Splendor active increases the character's Stellar Glimmer DMG by 15%. This lasts until her Dance Double exits the field or when she summons her Solo Dance Double again. When Odette is off-field, she loses 1 stack of Marvelous Splendor every second, while other nearby party members gain the corresponding number of Marvelous Splendor stacks at the same time.
Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling: Odette's special Elemental Skill, which deals to nearby opponents AoE Cryo DMG over time. Then, when the dance duel ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG. During the dance duet, Odette can control the direction of the dance steps. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The DMG dealt when the dance duet ends is changed to AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar reaction type. Additionally, after Odette unleashes the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling, should she be in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state while her Dance Double is still on the field, the Dance Double's two dance move types will also deal additional AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: Radiance: Stellar-Conduct and Radiance: Stellar Swirl are both considered Radiance: Stellar Glimmer states. Characters can only be affected by one Radiance state at any one time, and where more than one state can be triggered, Radiance: Stellar-Conduct shall apply first.
Decoding Power: When in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power will continuously increase. When its Decoding Power reaches the maximum, Fagio will be unable to maintain or re-enter Decoding mode.
Decoding: Fagio will switch to this mode when using a Charged Attack. When in this mode, Fagio will engage any opponents ahead with a sweeping fire attack, as well as fire condensed beams at intervals, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The condensed beams will instead deal AoE Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer DMG.
