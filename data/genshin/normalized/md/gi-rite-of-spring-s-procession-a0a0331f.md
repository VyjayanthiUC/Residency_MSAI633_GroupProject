# Rite of Spring's Procession

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Rite_of_Spring%27s_Procession | type=talent | effective=2026-09-23 -->


## Infobox

Name: Rite of Spring's Procession
Character: Vesna
Talent Type: 1st Ascension Passive
Category: Ascension
Description: After Vesna uses the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword or the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa! , she also gains 1 stack of Disciplinary Action which lasts 20s. Max 6 stacks, and the duration of each stack is independent of the others'. When she has Disciplinary Action stacks active, the Spirit Blades summoned by Vesna will deal DMG equal to (100% + 10% × Disciplinary Action stack count) of the original DMG instead. All active stacks of Disciplinary Action are cleared when Vesna uses the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory or when she leaves the field.

Rite of Spring's Procession is Vesna 's 1st Ascension Passive .


## Trivia

- The name of her 1st Ascension Passive is a reference to the ballet The Rite of Spring by Igor Stravinsky .

## Glossary

Windborne Sword: When in the Armed for Action mode, Vesna can consume Sword Essence to use her special Elemental Skill to deal Anemo DMG . Each use of the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword in the same instance of Armed for Action will increase the level of Windborne Sword, beginning from Lv. 1, up to Lv. 3 max. Using Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3 will also summon a Spirit Blade which deals Anemo DMG .
For the Tsaritsa!: Summons multiple Spirit Blades, dealing AoE Anemo DMG . Also gains a stack of Sword Essence.
Spirit Blade: A weapon with special power that Vesna can summon. Attacks the opponent alongside Vesna to deal Anemo DMG . Vesna can summon Spirit Blades through the following ways: By unleashing the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3; By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!; or By using Windborne Sword·: Transpose in the Constellation Unwavering Ardor. Radiance: Stellar Swirl: DMG dealt by the Spirit Blades become Anemo DMG that is considered Stellar Swirl reaction DMG instead.
