## Glossary

Spirit Blade: A weapon with special power that Vesna can summon. Attacks the opponent alongside Vesna to deal Anemo DMG . Vesna can summon Spirit Blades through the following ways: By unleashing the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3; By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!; or By using Windborne Sword·: Transpose in the Constellation Unwavering Ardor. Radiance: Stellar Swirl: DMG dealt by the Spirit Blades become Anemo DMG that is considered Stellar Swirl reaction DMG instead.
Sword Essence: The energy required to use the special Elemental Skill Windborne Swordwhile in the Armed for Action mode. Vesna can accumulate Sword Essence in the following ways: By performing a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, or Plunging attack while in the Armed for Action mode, or by unleashing Windborne Sword: Transpose from the Constellation Unwavering Ardor; By using the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory; or By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!
