## Adjustments & Optimizations

- Optimizes some of the Japanese voice acting for a cutscene in the Archon Quest " Of Myriad Paths, Flux, and Dissolution ." The optimized version of the voice acting will also be used in related storyline videos published by the official channels in the future.
- Optimizes the corresponding visual performance when Nicole stands still on sloped terrain where parts of her outfit may abnormally clip through the character model.
- Updated Map with indicator for which layer is currently the player is currently at and the layer indicator remains visible with landmark selected.
- Added setting for Quest dialogue to always allow single click to advance to the next line, even when there is a transition in progress.
- Added "Auto Switch Control Device in Main Menu" setting to automatically switch between controller mode and keyboard and mouse mode.
- Added keyboard shortcuts to some UI buttons, e.g. the Character screen.

Enemies:

- Optimizes the movement behavior of the enemy " Il Dottore " in the Trounce Domain " Binding Field of Universal Nirvana " in Co-Op Mode .

Quests:

- Optimizes dialogue progression in quests : In most quest dialogue sequences, Travelers will no longer need to wait for the current line to finish playing before pressing the screen to advance to the next line. This allows Travelers to progress through the story at their own pace. (This optimization will first be available in Version "Luna VIII" for the " Sunny Summer Fontinalia " and " Moon Gazing " quest chains. More new quests will support this feature in future versions.)

Audio:

- Optimizes the sound effects in certain interfaces and quests.
- Optimizes Chinese voice-over lines in certain quests.
- Optimizes part of the Japanese voice-over performance in a cutscene of the Archon Quest " Of Myriad Paths, Flux, and Dissolution ."
- Optimizes the wording of a Japanese voice-over line each for Escoffier , Columbina , and Nicole in the characters' Profile > Voice-Over interface.
- Optimizes the sound mixing for Mualani when she joins the party in the Party Setup interface.

System:

- Adds support for automatic switching between controller and keyboard-and-mouse controls in certain PC gameplay scenarios, in the open-world main interface. After "Auto Switch Control Device in Main Menu" is enabled under Settings > Controls , the game will automatically switch to the corresponding control mode based on the current input device (controller or keyboard and mouse).
- Adds support for keyboard shortcuts on certain screens on PC. (For example, on the Character screen, Travelers can use W/S to navigate up and down, Q/E to switch pages, R to open the Outfit screen, and F to level up characters.)

Exploration:

- Optimizes the display logic for the Multi-Layered Map button: The Multi-Layered Map button now indicate the Traveler's current layer. When the sidebar is open on the Map screen, the Multi-Layered Map button in the corresponding position will no longer be obscured and will remain visible alongside the sidebar.

## Known Issues

- There is currently an issue where certain related voice-over lines are missing when Columbina performs the first instance of her Normal Attack . This issue will be fixed in Version 7.0 .
- In certain interfaces or scenes, switching to characters wearing the new outfits (specifically Citlali 's " Whispers of Stars and Smoke " and Charlotte 's " Hurlock Variations ") may occasionally cause minor lags. This issue will be fixed in Version 7.0.
- On certain PC devices, switching to characters wearing the new outfits (specifically Citlali's "Whispers of Stars and Smoke" and Charlotte's "Hurlock Variations") in certain interfaces or scenes may occasionally cause serious in-game lag. Travelers on PC who encounter this issue can resolve it by disabling Compatibility Mode for both the game executable (Yuanshen.exe or GenshinImpact.exe) and the game launcher. *How to disable Compatibility Mode: Right-click the program icon, select "Properties" > "Compatibility", and uncheck "Run this program in compatibility mode for:". To locate the game executable, click the "≡" button in the bottom-right corner of the launcher interface, select "Game Settings," and click "Open Directory." If you have any questions, you may also contact Customer Service for guidance.
