## Infobox

Name: Stellar Swirl
Type: Elemental Reactions
Group: Stellar Glimmer Reactions

Stellar Swirl is the Stellar Glimmer Reaction triggered by inflicting Anemo on a target that is already affected by Cryo , replacing Cryo Swirl while a character with a corresponding Stellar Jubilee Passive is in the party . This reaction deals Anemo DMG and creates a Stellar Vortex that will explode after a period of time, dealing Cryo DMG . If Stellar Swirl is triggered with a Vortex nearby, its level will raise, causing it to deal increased DMG in a larger AoE.


## Damage

Main article: Damage § Stellar Glimmer Reaction Damage

When Stellar Swirl is triggered, an instance of Anemo DMG is dealt to the target opponent, and then a Lv. 1 Stellar Vortex is created that lasts for 3s . Stellar Vortices will explode after a delay, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . After triggering Stellar Swirl for the third time, the Vortex will level up to Lv. 2, after which it will deal greater DMG across a wider AoE upon bursting. If Stellar Swirl is triggered three more times, the Vortex will explode immediately.

Additionally, for 5s after a Stellar Vortex explodes, the player's next jump will have its height increased.


## Tutorial

Elemental Reaction: Stellar Swirl:
- When Anemo meets Cryo , and certain other conditions are met, a special Stellar Swirl reaction is triggered. This deals an instance of Anemo DMG to the opponent and generates a Stellar Vortex. Triggering Stellar Swirl multiple times raises the level of the Stellar Vortex, which explodes after a certain time or after a certain number of Stellar Swirl reactions have been triggered. This deals AoE Cryo DMG to nearby opponents. The aforementioned DMG can CRIT. Up to one Stellar Vortex can exist on the field within a certain range. A Stellar Vortex on the field will level up to Lv. 2 after a certain number of Stellar Swirl reactions have been triggered. A higher-level Vortex deals more AoE Cryo DMG to opponents over a wider area when it explodes.
After a Stellar Swirl reaction is triggered, certain nearby characters will also enter the Radiance: Stellar Swirl effect for 8s, which enhances certain Talents and skill effects. Certain characters can use their Talents to deal Stellar Swirl reaction DMG to opponents. This DMG can trigger CRIT Hits. When the Stellar Vortex explodes, it will also grant nearby characters the ability to jump higher once, for a short time.

## Glossary

Polestar Field: When Stellar-Conduct is triggered, the surrounding area will transform into a Polestar Field for a short period of time, and when inside a Polestar Field, certain characters will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state, which grants various skill buffs.
Solo Dance Double: A psychic double of Odette's that dances a duet with her. May be summoned to the field through her Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst. Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves to periodically attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . A maximum of one Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette is allowed on the field at any one time. Additionally, when there is a Dance Double summoned by Odette nearby, her Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers is changed to the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling.
Marvelous Splendor: After unlocking the Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One," Odette will obtain a special effect when she summons her Solo Dance Double. Every stack of Marvelous Splendor active increases the character's Stellar Glimmer DMG by 15%. This lasts until her Dance Double exits the field or when she summons her Solo Dance Double again. When Odette is off-field, she loses 1 stack of Marvelous Splendor every second, while other nearby party members gain the corresponding number of Marvelous Splendor stacks at the same time.
Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling: Odette's special Elemental Skill, which deals to nearby opponents AoE Cryo DMG over time. Then, when the dance duel ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG. During the dance duet, Odette can control the direction of the dance steps. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The DMG dealt when the dance duet ends is changed to AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar reaction type. Additionally, after Odette unleashes the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling, should she be in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state while her Dance Double is still on the field, the Dance Double's two dance move types will also deal additional AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.
