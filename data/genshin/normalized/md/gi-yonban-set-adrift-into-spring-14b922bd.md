## Infobox

Name: Yonban: Set Adrift into Spring
Character: Wanderer
Level: 4
Description: When casting Hanega: Song of the Wind , should the Ascension Talent " Jade-Claimed Flower " be triggered, the character will gain buffs in correspondence to the contacted Elemental Type(s), and also obtain a random untriggered buff. A maximum of 3 such corresponding elemental buffs can exist simultaneously. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Jade-Claimed Flower."
Previous: Sanban: Moonflower Kusemai
Next: Matsuban: Ancient Illuminator From Abroad

Yonban: Set Adrift into Spring is Wanderer 's Level 4 Constellation .
