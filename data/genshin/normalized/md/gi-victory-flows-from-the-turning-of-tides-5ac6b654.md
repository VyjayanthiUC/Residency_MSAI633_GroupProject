## Glossary

Phantasm Performance: When in the Shadow Dance state caused by her Elemental Skill Senet Strategy: Dance of a Thousand Nights, if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Nefer's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Phantasm Performance, which will not consume Stamina. Nefer summons shades of herself to perform Coordinated Attacks against enemies. Nefer and the shades will deal 2 and 3 stages of AoE Dendro DMG respectively. DMG dealt by the shades is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. 1 Verdant Dew will be consumed the first time shades are summoned after every Phantasm Performance.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
