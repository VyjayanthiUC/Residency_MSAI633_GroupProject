## Infobox

Name: Nature's Chorus
Character: Lauma
Talent Type: Moonsign Benediction Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When a Bloom reaction is triggered by a party member, it will be converted into the Lunar-Bloom reaction, with every point of Elemental Mastery that Lauma has increasing Lunar-Bloom's Base DMG by 0.0175%, up to a maximum of 14%.
