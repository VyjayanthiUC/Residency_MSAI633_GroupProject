## Infobox

Name: Koda: Sink With Thee
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: Elemental Burst
Category: Combat
Description: Leads a song of the Water Imps which lures its listeners to their doom. This deals AoE Hydro DMG to the opponent. When in the Song of Ages Past state, DMG dealt is further increased.
Energy Cost: 60
Cooldown: 15s

"According to legend, the song of the Water Imps can lead sailors to their ruin. But if everyone was lost to the water, where did this story come from?" "It's probably from a Domovoy sailor who was too short to go over the ship's railing, heh... Stop giving me that look, Odette! I was just joking!"

Koda: Sink With Thee is Vodyanitsa 's Elemental Burst .

## Glossary

Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn: Reveals her true Water Imp form, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and gaining theSong of Ages Past effect: While this effect is active, Vodyanitsa summons the ancientHorn of Spring's Call, periodically moving closer to an opponent in a swift movement to attack them, dealing Hydro DMG . The above two types of attacks will also decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of opponents hit. Additionally, while theSong of Ages Past effect is active, the party's active character gains increased resistance to interruption and periodically regenerates HP based on Vodyanitsa's Max HP.
