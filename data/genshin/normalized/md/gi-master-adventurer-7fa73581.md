## Infobox

Name: Master Adventurer
Character: Linnea
Talent Type: Utility Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When Linnea is not in combat, the charge time of her Aimed Shot is greatly decreased, and she will shoot an Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow instead. Different effects will occur depending on the targets hit: Specific animals: Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow will be transformed into a special "Net" that can capture the essence and form of these animals, using them as a medium to manifest the animal's physical form again. The "Net" can only be used for a limited number of times each week. Ore veins used in forging : Linnea will dispatch Lumi, who will quickly strike at the ore marked by the Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow, then collect the dislodged ore. If Lumi is not on the field, Linnea will summon her for a short duration to help obtain the ore. The DMG dealt to opponents by Linnea's Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow is reduced to 20% of her original Charged Arrow. Additionally, after firing a certain number of Ultra Convenient Quick Arrows within a short period of time, Linnea needs to take a temporary break before she can continue using them.

Master Adventurer is Linnea 's Utility Passive .

## Change History

[ Edit History ]

Version "Luna VII"

- The vertical range of Lumi's strike when dispatched by this Talent for what it can instantly mine was updated to be the same as what it can damage. Previously, the strike had a greater vertical range for what it could damage than for what it could instantly mine, which caused Lumi to damage higher and lower ore on sloped terrain without instantly mining it.

- Master Adventurer was released.
