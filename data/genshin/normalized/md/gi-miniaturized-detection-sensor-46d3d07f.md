Miniaturized Detection Sensor is Aino 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Aino must be in the active party for Miniaturized Detection Sensor to take effect, but she does not need to be alive.
- Only one local specialty will be shown on the mini-map at a time.
- The following 2 Talents are identical to this talent: A Light in the Dark ( Flins ) Prayers for the Forest ( Lauma )
- A Light in the Dark ( Flins )
- Prayers for the Forest ( Lauma )
- Additionally, the appearance of the Cool Your Jets Ducky can be altered by purchasing Aino's Toolbox from the Workshop's Trove .
