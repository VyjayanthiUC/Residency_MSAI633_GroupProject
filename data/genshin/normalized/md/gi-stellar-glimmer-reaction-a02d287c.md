# Stellar Glimmer Reaction

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stellar_Glimmer_Reaction | type=mechanic | effective=2026-07-01 -->


## Infobox

Name: Stellar Glimmer Reaction
Type: Combat Game Systems
Group: Elemental Reactions

Stellar Glimmer Reactions are a subset of Elemental Reactions .

In order to trigger a Stellar Glimmer Reaction , a character with a passive talent that converts specific Cryo -related elemental reactions must be present in the party .

Stellar Glimmer reactions will also interrupt a specific attack from Snowland Fae and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters .


## List of Stellar Glimmer Reactions

- Stellar-Conduct
- Stellar Swirl
