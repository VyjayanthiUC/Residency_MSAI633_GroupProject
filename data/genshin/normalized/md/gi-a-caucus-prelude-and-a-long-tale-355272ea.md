## Infobox

Description: When Sandrone is in the party , you will gain additional snack rewards after turning in your Daily Commissions with Katheryne.

A Caucus Prelude and a Long Tale is Sandrone 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Sandrone must be in the active party for A Caucus Prelude and a Long Tale to take effect, but she does not need to be alive.
- Rewards one of the following: Tea Party Prelude: Artful Latte Tea Party Prelude: Herbal Tea
- Tea Party Prelude: Artful Latte
- Tea Party Prelude: Herbal Tea
