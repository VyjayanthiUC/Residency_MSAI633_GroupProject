utility1: Utility Expedition Nod-Krai Expedition Length Reduction

Backstreet Guile is Jahoda 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- This passive grants Jahoda access to the Intel Hunt feature, which is only available to members of the Curatorium of Secrets . When Jahoda is near certain NPCs , her Elemental Skill will be replaced with a special intel-gathering skill. Using it on these NPCs will reveal additional information about them and potentially unlock exclusive dialogue and/or quests . For each NPC, gathering intel from them for the first time will reward Recorded Intel ×1 . Recorded Intel can be exchanged with Katheryne to unlock Intel Quests . The intel-gathering skill has a fixed cooldown of 8.5 seconds, but uses Jahoda's standard Elemental Skill animation. Curatorium members can gather intel from a combined total of 6 new NPCs per Weekly Reset . Attempting to gather more intel with Jahoda will trigger the following dialogue: Media:vo mwgwq102 1 jahoda 09.ogg (All done! Time to head back for some Ashru time, hehe...)
