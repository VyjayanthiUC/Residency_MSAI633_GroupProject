- Decreased the requirements for the achievements QUEST CLEAR and Hero-in-Training .
- Adjusted claiming Daily Commission rewards to give option to exchange Encounter Points .
- Tracking in Adventurer Handbook > Enemies now shows multiple targets and shows more spawn locations for certain enemies.

- Optimized penalty system to send mail when recently reported account banned and banned accounts have a ban icon on their profile avatar.
- Adjusted Battle Pass missions for Imaginarium Theater to carry over to next version of cleared for the month.

- Optimizes the combat experience when challenging the enemy " Experimental Field Generator ": Characters can now deal damage to it during the brief animation in which it deploys its Gravity Reduction Field.

- In the "Lock Assistance > Set Lock Plan" interface, clicking a Set icon displays the effects of that Artifact Set .

Battle Pass:

- The Battle Pass system's "This BP Period" has been updated to include cross-version tracking for Imaginarium Theater task progress: When the Battle Pass is available after a version update, if you've cleared and completed all 8 acts of that month's Imaginarium Theater, the corresponding missions will automatically complete.

## Post-patch Optimization & Fixes

2025-12-05 10:10 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue in Russian whereby the description text of Jahoda 's Lv. 2 Constellation had a display error, whereby the "click underlined text to show 'Related Talents'" function failed to take effect normally.
- Fixes an issue in the English "Character > Profile > Voice-Over" interface whereby the text for Durin 's voice-over "About Durin: Growing Up" and Jahoda's voice-over "Jahoda's Hobbies" were inconsistent with the actual English voice-overs.

2025-12-05 23:15 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue whereby using the " Quick Start " function to start Durin's Story Quest " The Magic Known as Storytelling " would result in an abnormal "Adventure Rank too low" message displaying on the quest interface under certain circumstances, preventing the quest from continuing.

2025-12-08 14:55 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue whereby Durin would not appear normally in the Theater Lobby or would not be displayed as a companion in the Serenitea Pot after changing into the outfit " Toward the Distant Horizon ."

2025-12-11 15:20 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue whereby unlocked Boss enemies were not displayed in the " Enemies " section of the Adventurer Handbook after the Version "Luna III" update.
- Fixes an issue whereby, under certain circumstances, the Silver Isotomas generated by Albedo 's additional Talent could create Transient Blossoms with an abnormal cooldown.
- Fixes an issue whereby, under certain circumstances, Durin would fall abnormally when casting his Special Elemental Skill " Transmutation: Confirmation of Purity ."
- Optimizes the character overview description for Venti in the " Test Run " event.
- Reduces the difficulty of combat against the enemy " Iniquitous Baptist " in the Normal, Advancing, Hard, and Menacing difficulties of the " Stygian Onslaught " event.
- Fixes an issue in Miliastra Wonderland whereby permanent "Wonderland Gift Boxes" that were completed in the previous season would incorrectly appear as "Not Completed" in the Gift Box Shop after a season update.
- Fixes an issue in the " Odes " interface of Miliastra Wonderland whereby the Manekin displaying the " Frosty Plume " outfit would exhibit slight abnormal jittering in its silhouette.
- Fixes an issue in the " Gameplay Guide " interface of Miliastra Wonderland whereby, after switching tabs, Travelers at Lv. 11 and above would have their " Wonderland Level " interface effects incorrectly disappear.

Travelers affected by the above issues can log out via the Paimon Menu by selecting "Exit to Login Interface" and close the game, then log in again to resolve the issues.

2025-12-18 15:15 UTC+8:

- Updates resources related to subsequent events.

2025-12-19 19:30 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue whereby the "Match with team players when playing solo" matchmaking setting in the "Snowtrace“ gameplay of the " Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey " event would be disabled by default.

2025-12-25 15:10 UTC+8:

- Fixes an issue under certain circumstances in the "Trial of Valor" gameplay of the " Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey " event whereby there was a small chance that Trial Characters not matching the current stage would appear on the team configuration interface, preventing Travelers from starting the Challenge. Travelers affected by this issue can log out via the Paimon Menu by selecting "Exit to Login Interface" and close the game, then log in again to resolve the issue.
