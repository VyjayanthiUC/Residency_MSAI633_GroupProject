Vesna Strivozha is a playable Anemo character in Genshin Impact .

Commander of the Druzhna , she is considered the Tsaritsa 's right hand and is often by her side, along with the head housemaid of the Snezhnayan royal household, Danica .

## Glossary

Armed for Action: Vesna switches to this mode after using the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory. In this mode, which lasts up to 15s: Vesna's resistance to interruption is increased. DMG dealt by Vesna with her Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, and Plunging Attacks becomes Anemo DMG that cannot be overridden by another elemental infusion instead. She will also summon a wind pinion that attacks the opponent at the same time, dealing additional Anemo DMG . Each time the wind pinion is summoned, Vesna will regenerate a certain amount of Sword Essence. Vesna can consume Sword Essence to unleash the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword, which deals Anemo DMG to her opponents. Vesna will exit the mode when its duration ends or after she has used Windborne Sword at the highest level 3 times. Exiting will clear all remaining Sword Essence she has accrued.
Sword Essence: The energy required to use the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword while in the Armed for Action mode. Vesna can accumulate Sword Essence in the following ways: By performing a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, or Plunging attack while in the Armed for Action mode, or by unleashing Windborne Sword: Transpose from the Constellation Unwavering Ardor; By using the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory; or By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!
Windborne Sword: When in the Armed for Action mode, Vesna can consume Sword Essence to use her special Elemental Skill to deal Anemo DMG . Each use of the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword in the same instance of Armed for Action will increase the level of Windborne Sword, beginning from Lv. 1, up to Lv. 3 max. Using Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3 will also summon a Spirit Blade which deals Anemo DMG .
Spirit Blade: A weapon with special power that Vesna can summon. Attacks the opponent alongside Vesna to deal Anemo DMG . Vesna can summon Spirit Blades through the following ways: By unleashing the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3; By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!; or By using Windborne Sword·: Transpose in the Constellation Unwavering Ardor. Radiance: Stellar Swirl: DMG dealt by the Spirit Blades become Anemo DMG that is considered Stellar Swirl reaction DMG instead.
For the Tsaritsa!: Summons multiple Spirit Blades, dealing AoE Anemo DMG . Also gains a stack of Sword Essence.
Rite of Spring's Procession: After Vesna uses the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword or the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!, she also gains 1 stack of Disciplinary Action which lasts 20s. Max 6 stacks, and the duration of each stack is independent of the others'. When she has Disciplinary Action stacks active, the Spirit Blades summoned by Vesna will deal DMG equal to (100% + 10% × Disciplinary Action stack count) of the original DMG instead. All active stacks of Disciplinary Action are cleared when Vesna uses the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory or when she leaves the field.
The Art of Victory: Calls forth Spirit Blades passed down through generations of the Vila to deal AoE Anemo DMG , then switches to the Armed for Action mode and gains 2 stacks of Sword Essence mode. Vesna can accumulate Sword Essence in the following ways: By performing a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, or Plunging attack while in the Armed for Action mode, or by unleashing Windborne Sword: Transpose from the Constellation Unwavering Ardor; By using the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory; or By using the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!. Armed for Action DMG dealt by Vesna with her Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, and Plunging Attacks become Anemo DMG that cannot be overridden by another elemental infusion instead. She will also summon a wind pinion that attacks the opponent at the same time, dealing additional Anemo DMG . Each time the wind pinion is summoned, Vesna will regenerate a certain amount of Sword Essence. Vesna can consume Sword Essence to unleash the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword, which deals to the opponent Anemo DMG . Each use of the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword in the same instance of Armed for Action will increase the level of Windborne Sword, beginning from Lv. 1 to Lv. 3 max, for more Anemo DMG . Using Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3 will also summon Spirit Blades which deal Anemo DMG . Vesna will exit the mode when its duration ends or when she has used Windborne Sword at the highest level 3 times. Exiting will clear all remaining Sword Essence she has accrued. Additionally, when in this mode, Vesna's resistance to interruption is increased. Radiance: Stellar Swirl: DMG dealt with the Spirit Blades becomes Anemo DMG that is also considered Stellar Swirl reaction DMG.
Truth Prevails: Radiance: Stellar Swirl: Vesna will gain the corresponding effect based on the Elemental Type of her party members: For every Cryo or Anemo character in her current party, Vesna's ATK increases by 6%; and For every character of all other Elemental Types in her current party, Vesna's Elemental Mastery increases by 25 instead.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
