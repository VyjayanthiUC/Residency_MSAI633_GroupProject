Masque of the Red Death When Arlecchino has a Bond of Life equal to or greater than 30% of her Max HP , she will enter the "Masque of the Red Death" state, where her Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks will be converted to deal Pyro DMG . This will consume 7.5% of said current Bond of Life. Her Bond of Life can be consumed this way every 0.03s. When her Bond of Life is consumed in this manner, All Is Ash 's CD will decrease by 0.8s.

## Gameplay Notes

- Unlike all other Polearm users, Arlecchino's Charged Attack is not preceded by a Normal Attack.
- The third hit of Arlecchino's Normal Attack will pull enemies towards her.
- If Arlecchino wields any other polearm other than Crimson Moon's Semblance , her polearm will have a yellow scythe blade starting from the second hit of her Normal Attack combo.
- While holding her Charged Attack and during her high speed movement, she will sprout a red wing and be able to fly over the surface of water .
- Her Normal Attack AoE is increased during her "Masque of the Red Death" state.
- Arlecchino remains in the "Masque of the Red Death" state even if she is swapped off field and swapped back in, so long as her Bond of Life is greater than 30% Max HP.
- During the "Masque of the Red Death" state, the base damage of Normal Attacks is calculated as: ATK × ( Talent Multiplier + Masque of the Red Death Increase × Bond of Life Max HP ) {\displaystyle {\text{ATK}}\times ({\text{Talent Multiplier}}+{\text{Masque of the Red Death Increase}}\times {\frac {\text{Bond of Life}}{\text{Max HP}}})}
- The on-hit 7.5% Bond of Life consumption during the "Masque of the Red Death" state is multiplicative.

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 1, "All Reprisals and Arrears, Mine to Bear..." : "Masque of the Red Death is further enhanced, the value of the increase is 100%. Additionally, Arlecchino's interruption resistance is increased when she performs Normal or Charged Attacks while affected by the Masque of the Red Death."
- Constellation Level 3, "You Shall Become a New Member of Our Family..." : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
