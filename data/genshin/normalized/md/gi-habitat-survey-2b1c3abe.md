Name: Habitat Survey
Character: Linnea
Talent Type: Moonsign Benediction Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When a party member triggers a Hydro Crystallize reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Crystallize reaction, with every 100 DEF that Linnea has increasing Lunar-Crystallize's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%.
