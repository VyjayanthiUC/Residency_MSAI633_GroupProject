## Glossary

Lead Vocal: When the party's currently active character deals Hydro DMG or Cryo DMG with a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, Plunging Attack, Elemental Skill, or Elemental Burst, 1 stack of "Lead Vocal" is consumed. This in turn increases the DMG dealt. However, if a Wandering Vortex is present on the field or during the 5s following a Wandering Vortex explosion, this effect will only trigger when the character deals Stellar Swirl reaction DMG. Every 1000 Max HP Vodyanitsa has in excess of 40000 will increase the Stellar Swirl reaction DMG from the above effect by 260, as well as Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG dealt by 140. Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt can be increased by a maximum of 6500 while Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG can be increased by a maximum of 3500 in this way.
Chorus: Triggered by off-field party members or other nearby party members (and not your currently active party member). Effects are the same as those of "Lead Vocal."
