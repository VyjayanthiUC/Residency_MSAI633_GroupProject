# Splendid Prelude

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Splendid_Prelude | type=talent | effective=2026-09-23 -->


## Infobox

Name: Splendid Prelude
Character: Vesna
Talent Type: Stellar Jubilee Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When party members trigger Cryo Swirl, it will be changed to a Stellar Swirl reaction, and party members' Base DMG for the Stellar Swirl will be increased based on Vesna's ATK. Every 100 ATK she has increases the Base Stellar Swirl DMG by 0.7%. The maximum DMG increase that can be gained this way is 14%. After a party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction nearby, Vesna enters the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s.

Splendid Prelude is Vesna 's Stellar Jubilee Passive .
