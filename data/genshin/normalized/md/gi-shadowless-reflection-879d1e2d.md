## Infobox

Name: Shadowless Reflection
Character: Illuga
Talent Type: Elemental Burst
Category: Combat
Description: Light the lamp, dealing AoE Geo DMG , and gain Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect for 20s. When this effect is active, Illuga gains 21 stacks of Nightingale's Song . When Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, Plunging Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts of nearby active party members deal Geo DMG to opponents, 1 stack of Nightingale’s Song is consumed to increase the DMG dealt. Increase in DMG is based on Illuga’s Elemental Mastery. If DMG is inflicted by Lunar-Crystallize , DMG dealt will increase further. When Geo DMG dealt in this manner hits multiple opponents, multiple stacks of Nightingale's Song will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit. Additionally, if there are Geo Constructs created by nearby party members on the battlefield while Illuga unleashes his Elemental Burst, or when nearby party members create Geo Constructs while Haunted Night's Oriole-Song is active, every Geo Construct on the battlefield will allow Illuga to gain 5 additional stacks of Nightingale's Song. Within 20s after unleashing his Elemental Burst, Illuga can gain a maximum of 15 additional stacks of Nightingale’s Song this way. When all stacks of Nightingale's Song have been consumed, or when the state's duration ends, the Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect will end.

## Gameplay Notes

Passive Effects:

- Passive Talent 1, Torchforger's Covenant : "After unleashing Elemental Skill Dawnbearing Songbird or Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection , nearby party members will gain the Lightkeeper's Oath effect for 20s: For Geo DMG dealt to opponents, CRIT Rate increases by 5% and CRIT DMG increases by 10%. Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam When party members are affected by the Lightkeeper's Oath effect, Elemental Mastery is increased by 50."
- Passive Talent 4, Demonhunter's Dusk : " Nightingale's Song 's effects are enhanced: When there are 1/2/3 Hydro or Geo characters in the party, Nightingale's Song will further increase DMG. Increase in DMG is equal to 7%/14%/24% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery. If this aforementioned DMG is inflicted by a Lunar-Crystallize reaction, increase in DMG is equal to 48%/96%/160% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery."

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 2, Elk With Fanged Antlers : "When Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection 's Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect is active, for every 7 stacks of Nightingale's Song consumed, Illuga will summon Aedon, dealing a single instance of Geo DMG to 1 nearby opponent. The DMG dealt is based on 400% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery and 200% of his DEF. This DMG is considered Elemental Burst DMG."
- Constellation Level 3, Earthshaking Maw : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
- Constellation Level 4, Solarhunting Wolf : "When Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection 's Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect is active, nearby active party members will have their DEF increased by 200."

## Glossary

Nightingale's Song: The effect gained after Illuga unleashes his Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection . It can increase the Geo DMG inflicted by current active characters.
Dawnbearing Songbird: Summons the trusty messenger bird, 'Aedon.' Different effects are produced depending on whether the skill is Tapped or Held. Tap Aedon dashes towards enemies in front, dealing Geo DMG to enemies it comes into contact with. Hold Enter aiming mode, and command Aedon to dash towards opponents being aimed at, dealing Geo DMG to opponents it comes into contact with.
Shadowless Reflection: Light the lamp, dealing AoE Geo DMG , and gain Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect for 20s. When this effect is active, Illuga gains 21 stacks of Nightingale's Song. It can increase the Geo DMG inflicted by current active characters. When Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, Plunging Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts of nearby active party members deal Geo DMG to opponents, 1 stack of Nightingale’s Song is consumed to increase the DMG dealt. Increase in DMG is based on Illuga’s Elemental Mastery. If DMG is inflicted by Lunar-Crystallize, DMG dealt will increase further. When Geo DMG dealt in this manner hits multiple opponents, multiple stacks of Nightingale's Song will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit. Additionally, if there are Geo Constructs created by nearby party members on the battlefield while Illuga unleashes his Elemental Burst, or when nearby party members create Geo Constructs while Haunted Night's Oriole-Song is active, every Geo Construct on the battlefield will allow Illuga to gain 5 additional stacks of Nightingale's Song. Within 20s after unleashing his Elemental Burst, Illuga can gain a maximum of 15 additional stacks of Nightingale’s Song this way. When all stacks of Nightingale's Song have been consumed, or when the state's duration ends, the Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect will end.
