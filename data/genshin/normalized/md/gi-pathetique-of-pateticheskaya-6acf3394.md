Pathetique of Pateticheskaya is Odette 's 4th Ascension Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- The DMG increase increases the Base DMG Multiplier directly, and is not a Reaction Base DMG Bonus . Consequently, it only applies to Stellar Glimmer DMG dealt directly by Odette, and not any indirect Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG she contributes to.

## Trivia

- The name of her 4th Ascension Passive is a reference to the Pathétique Symphony (Symphony No. 6 in B Minor) ( Russian : Патетическая Pateticheskaya ) by Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky .
