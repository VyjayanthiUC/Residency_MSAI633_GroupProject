## Official Introduction

Discipline Down to the Second

"Oh, that officer? Don't go thinking all he does is chase me around announcing the time and nagging me to get back to work. If anyone threatens the Armory Palace's projects or researchers, you'll get to witness a terrifying sight: 'a fortress going on the offensive and rolling right over people."

— Mitya

The Armory Palace is a vast research, design, and production complex within Glupov . As Snezhnaya's foremost research institution, it has developed numerous cutting-edge technologies and many of the weapons that form the backbone of the snowbound nation's military. The secrets and valuable assets in its keeping are beyond counting.

To protect the Armory Palace and its personnel, elite troops from the 1st Grenadier Regiment were reorganized into the Special Assignments Battalion, serving as the institution's dedicated guard under the command of Major Valeriy.

His service record is particularly distinguished. He has led his troops in wiping out six concentrations of monsters linked to Ley Line disturbances, crushing three vicious Volkodlak gangs, and eliminating more than eighty percent of the rampaging chimeric monsters west of Snezhnograd ...

He also schedules the entire battalion's duties with unfailing precision, day after day, year after year... all without an adjutant's assistance.

With an expert in both combat and administration commanding their guard, the Armory Palace's specialists can devote themselves to research and production with complete peace of mind.

Indeed, since the Special Assignments Battalion took up its post, Major Valeriy and his troops have neutralized more than ninety threats of every description, including surviving bandits plotting to steal the Armory Palace's secrets and outlaws attempting to abduct its researchers.

In recent years, however, military observers have noted something surprising: Major Valeriy has become a frequent visitor to the Korolevskiy Theater and Podvorye Restaurant , among other entertainment venues...

Major Valeriy declines to comment on any of the speculation. He continues to let his impeccable time management and faultless organization at work speak for themselves.

People are not machines, of course. They cannot be expected to run like clockwork, keeping to a rigid schedule every moment of the day. But sound scheduling is a safeguard. When the unexpected disrupts a plan, it provides the room to adapt and the confidence to do so.

There will always be work that calls for contingency plans, and deadlines that simply cannot be missed.

And someone must always bear the strain, keep a constant eye on the schedule, and head off delays and mishaps.
