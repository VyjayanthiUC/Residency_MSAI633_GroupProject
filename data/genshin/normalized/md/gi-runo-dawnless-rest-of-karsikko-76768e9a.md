The sagas of the scions of the far north tell of how the apostle of the golden city and the emissary of the gods once used the "primordial tongue" to shape the forms of all mortal things. Thus were the hymns known as Runo — imbued with power and sacred authority — established as the very foundation of magic. Though the words of those first incantations are now lost to time, their melodies — and the memories woven within them — have been passed down, and the powers they awaken, though faint, remain capable of stirring change.

Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko is Lauma 's Elemental Skill .

- Constellation Level 6, "I Offer Blood and Tears to the Moonlight" : "When the Frostgrove Sanctuary attacks opponents, it will deal 1 additional instance of AOE Dendro DMG equal to 185% of Lauma's Elemental Mastery . This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. This instance of DMG will not consume any Pale Hymn stacks and will provide Lauma with 2 stacks of Pale Hymn, as well as refreshing the duration of Pale Hymn stacks gained in this manner. This effect can occur up to 8 times during each Frostgrove Sanctuary. When using the Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko , all Pale Hymn stacks gained in this manner will be removed. Additionally, when Lauma uses a Normal Attack while she has Pale Hymn stacks, she will consume 1 stack to convert this to deal Dendro DMG equal to 150% of her Elemental Mastery. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam : All nearby party members' Lunar-Bloom DMG is elevated by 25%."

## Trivia

- Runo is a form of traditional Finnish poetry .
- In the Finnic religion , a karsikko was a set of markings made on a tree somewhere between a deceased person's home and the burial site, which was believed to prevent the individual's spirit from coming back.

## Glossary

Frostgrove Sanctuary: A magical array that protects the Moonchanters and can be summoned by using the Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko. The Frostgrove Sanctuary will follow the active character, dealing a single instance of AoE Dendro DMG to opponents within the array every 2s. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
Verdant Dew: When party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party will receive such a resource. Your party will receive 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s for 2.5s after triggering a Lunar-Bloom reaction. Your party can have up to 3 Verdant Dew. If you trigger another Lunar-Bloom reaction during this time, the duration of this effect will be reset.
Moon Song: A melody used to weave together hymns. When an Elemental Burst is used, or for a period of time after an Elemental Burst has been used, it can be converted into Pale Hymn stacks that increase the DMG from Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, and Lunar-Bloom reactions. When Lauma consumes Verdant Dew to perform the Hold version of her Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko, each Verdant Dew consumed will provide Lauma with 1 stack of Moon Song.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
