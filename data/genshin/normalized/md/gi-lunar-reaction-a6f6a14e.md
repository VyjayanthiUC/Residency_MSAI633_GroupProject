# Lunar Reaction

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Lunar_Reaction | type=mechanic | effective=None -->


## Infobox

Name: Lunar Reaction
Type: Combat Game Systems
Group: Elemental Reactions

Lunar Reactions are a subset of Elemental Reactions .

In order to trigger a Lunar Reaction , a character with a Moonsign Benediction passive talent that converts specific Hydro -related elemental reactions must be present in the party .

Lunar Reactions, unlike their base variants, can inflict CRIT Hits .


## List of Lunar Reactions

- Lunar-Bloom
- Lunar-Charged
- Lunar-Crystallize
