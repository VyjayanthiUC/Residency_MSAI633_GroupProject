## Elemental Resonance

Elemental Resonance redirects here. For other uses, see Resonance .

Current Party Elemental Types

When you have a full party, you will receive Elemental Resonance effects that correspond to the Elemental Types present in the party.


## Elemental Resonance › Effects

Elemental Resonance effects depend on the elements of the first four party members, apply in Co-Op Mode , and do not require all party members to be alive.

Except for Protective Canopy, each Resonance requires two or more characters of the same element among the first four party slots. Having more than two characters of the same element does not further enhance the corresponding Resonance.

In Co-Op Mode, if part of the party is inside a Domain , the resonance will only be determined by the characters that are in the Domain, and only characters in the Domain will benefit from the bonuses.

## Moonsign

Moonsign

When Moonsign characters are in the party, the party will attain a Moonsign Level corresponding to the number of such characters. When the party reaches "Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam," Moonsign characters will be enhanced. Non-Moonsign characters can also provide buffs to the party.


## Moonsign › Moonsign Characters

Aino; Columbina; Flins; Illuga; Ineffa; Jahoda; Lauma; Linnea; Nefer; Zibai;


## Moonsign › Effects

| Name | Moonsign characters | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Nascent Gleam | 1 | All characters in the party will gain the "Moonsign: Nascent Gleam" effect. |
| Ascendant Gleam | 2 | All characters in the party will gain the "Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam" effect, enhancing certain Talents and Skills of Moonsign characters. Unless otherwise stated, when Moonsign Level reaches Ascendant Gleam, the effects of Nascent Gleam will also apply. In addition, for 20s after a non-Moonsign character casts an Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst , they will increase the Lunar Reaction DMG dealt by all nearby characters based on their own Elemental Type , up to a maximum of 36%. This effect cannot stack. If the character's Elemental Type is: Pyro , Electro , or Cryo : Increases this DMG by 0.9% for every 100 ATK . Hydro : Increases this DMG by 0.6% for every 1,000 Max HP . Geo : Increases this DMG by 1% for every 100 DEF . Anemo or Dendro : Increases this DMG by 2.25% for every 100 Elemental Mastery . |


## Moonsign › Ascendant Gleam Buff Thresholds

Character stats required to reach maximum Ascendant Gleam Lunar Reaction DMG buff:

- Pyro , Electro , or Cryo : 4,000 ATK
- Hydro : 60,000 Max HP
- Geo : 3,600 DEF
- Anemo or Dendro : 1,600 Elemental Mastery


## Hexerei

Hexerei

Obtain the Hexerei: Secret Rite effect when you have at least two Hexerei characters in your party. This effect will further enhance your Hexerei characters.


## Hexerei › Hexerei Characters

## Region-Based Effects

The following effects are affected by each character's associated region in the party. Unlike Elemental Resonance, Moonsign, and Hexerei, these effects are not listed on the Party Setup menu.


## Region-Based Effects › Nightsoul Burst

Main article: Nightsoul's Blessing § Nightsoul Burst

When there is at least one Natlanese character in the party, a Nightsoul Burst can be triggered at set intervals. For every Natlanese characters in the party, the interval between Nightsoul Bursts will decrease: 18 seconds for 1 character, 12 seconds for 2 characters, and 9 seconds for 3 or more characters. Characters like Bennett or a Pyro-aligned Traveler do not count toward enabling Nightsoul Burst or decreasing its cooldown, even if they are canonically from Natlan or are able to enter the Nightsoul's Blessing state, respectively.
