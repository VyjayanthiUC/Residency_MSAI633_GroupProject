## Infobox

Name: Moondew Cascade
Character: Columbina
Talent Type: Normal Attack
Category: Combat
Description: Normal Attack Summons Moonlit Tides, performing up to 3 attacks that deal Hydro DMG . Charged Attack Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Hydro DMG after a short casting time. If you have at least 1 Verdant Dew , Columbina's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Moondew Cleanse , which will not consume Stamina: Consume 1 Verdant Dew to deal 3 instances of AoE Dendro DMG to opponents in front. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Plunging Attack Plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Hydro DMG upon impact with the ground.
scale_att1: Scaling ATK Max HP
utility1: Utility Direct Lunar-Bloom

Moondew Cascade is Columbina 's Normal Attack .


## Gameplay Notes

- As long as Columbina has sufficient Verdant Dew or Moonridge Dew , the Normal Attack button can be held to cast Moondew Cleanse multiple times in a row.

## Glossary

Verdant Dew: When party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party will receive such a resource. Your party will receive 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s for 2.5s after triggering a Lunar-Bloom reaction. Your party can have up to 3 Verdant Dew. If you trigger another Lunar-Bloom reaction during this time, the duration of this effect will be reset.
Moonridge Dew: A special Verdant Dew resource provided by Columbina's Passive Talent, Law of the New Moon. Its maximum capacity and duration are calculated separately from Verdant Dew. When the party's Verdant Dew is expended, Moonridge Dew will be consumed instead. Your party can have up to 3 Moonridge Dew.
