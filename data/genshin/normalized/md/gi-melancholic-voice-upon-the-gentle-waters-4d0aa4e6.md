Name: Melancholic Voice Upon the Gentle Waters
Character: Vodyanitsa
Level: 4
Description: When Vodyanitsa triggers the healing effect of Song of Ages Past , an additional effect will be triggered based on the healed character's HP. If the character's HP is below 40%: The healing is increased by 50%; and If the character's HP is 40% or above: Vodyanitsa's Max HP is increased by 20% for 6s. Max 3 stacks, and each stack's duration is counted independently.

The above two types of attacks will also decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of opponents hit. Additionally, while the Song of Ages Past effect is active, the party's active character gains increased resistance to interruption and periodically regenerates HP based on Vodyanitsa's Max HP.
