## Infobox

Name: Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers
Character: Odette
Talent Type: Elemental Skill
Category: Combat
Description: With slow, graceful dance steps, Odette deals AoE Cryo DMG to the opponent, and also summons her Solo Dance Double to the field. If a Dance Double summoned by Odette is already on the field, this will re-summon the Dance Double and reset its duration. Solo Dance Double Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves, periodically attacking nearby opponents and dealing to them AoE Cryo DMG . Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers, Odette's Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will become the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling instead, where a dance duet deals AoE Cryo DMG to nearby opponents over time. Then, when the duet ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl DMG.
utility1: Utility Direct Stellar-Conduct Direct Stellar Swirl
Cooldown: 15s

"Dance, o candle flame of winter, dance!" "Pain and tears, laughter and happiness... Life, o life! Life is but a fleeting solo dance: one is only briefly alive with music and mirth before they fade into silence as the curtain falls..."

Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers is Odette 's Elemental Skill .


## Gameplay Notes

- When Skill DMG or Coda at Dawn's Tolling DoT hits at least one enemy , it generates 5 Elemental Particles . This particle generation has a cooldown of 12 seconds.
- This particle generation has a cooldown of 12 seconds.
- Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling deals three instances of AoE Cryo DMG before the Cryo Stellar Glimmer finisher.
- Solo Dance Double will perform one dance move every 2s .

## Trivia

- Adagio is a ballet and music term (from Italian Adagio or French adage ) meaning "slowly, at ease".
- The name of her Elemental Skill is a reference to the drama Giselle, or the Phantom Night Dancers written by William Thomas Moncrieff , based on the ballet Giselle by Adolphe Adam .

## Glossary

Solo Dance Double: A psychic double of Odette's that dances a duet with her. May be summoned to the field through her Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst. Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves to periodically attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . A maximum of one Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette is allowed on the field at any one time. Additionally, when there is a Dance Double summoned by Odette nearby, her Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers is changed to the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling.
Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling: Odette's special Elemental Skill, which deals to nearby opponents AoE Cryo DMG over time. Then, when the dance duel ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG. During the dance duet, Odette can control the direction of the dance steps. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The DMG dealt when the dance duet ends is changed to AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar reaction type. Additionally, after Odette unleashes the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling, should she be in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state while her Dance Double is still on the field, the Dance Double's two dance move types will also deal additional AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.
