## Infobox

Name: Narcissus Wakes, Her Eyes Upon the Dawn
Character: Sandrone
Level: 6
Description: When Fagio fires a condensed beam for the third time while in Decoding mode, the beam becomes a more powerful Condensed Cluster Beam that fires continuously. This beam will deal up to 4 additional instances of AoE Cryo DMG at 100% of Sandrone's ATK on top of any subsequent original beam fired. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The Condensed Cluster Beam will instead deal 4 additional instances of AoE Cryo DMG . The DMG dealt with this attack, which is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG, is equal to: Radiance: Stellar-Conduct : 80% of Sandrone's ATK. Radiance: Stellar Swirl : 120% of Sandrone's ATK. Additionally, all Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by Sandrone is elevated by 20%.

## Glossary

Decoding: Fagio will switch to this mode when using a Charged Attack. When in this mode, Fagio will engage any opponents ahead with a sweeping fire attack, as well as fire condensed beams at intervals, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The condensed beams will instead deal AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
