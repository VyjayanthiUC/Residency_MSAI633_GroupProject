## Infobox

Name: Presto: Bluebird Finale
Character: Odette
Talent Type: Elemental Burst
Category: Combat
Description: With quick, lively dance steps, Odette deals multiple instances of AoE Cryo DMG , and summons her Solo Dance Double . She also gains Snow Swan's Dream, which increases the Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG Odette deals. Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Burst Presto: Bluebird Finale, Odette's Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will be replaced with the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling instead. If there is a Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette on the field, it will be summoned to her side with its duration refreshed.
Energy Cost: 60
Cooldown: 15s

The dawn-lit clouds were hanging just within reach. It was as though she needed only spread her wings to touch them.

Presto: Bluebird Finale is Odette 's Elemental Burst .

## Trivia

- Presto is an Italian music term meaning "very fast".
- The name of her Elemental Burst is a reference to the Bluebird Pas de Deux from the ballet The Sleeping Beauty by Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky .

## Glossary

Solo Dance Double: A psychic double of Odette's that dances a duet with her. May be summoned to the field through her Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst. Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves to periodically attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . A maximum of one Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette is allowed on the field at any one time. Additionally, when there is a Dance Double summoned by Odette nearby, her Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers is changed to the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling.
Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling: Odette's special Elemental Skill, which deals to nearby opponents AoE Cryo DMG over time. Then, when the dance duel ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct DMG. During the dance duet, Odette can control the direction of the dance steps. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The DMG dealt when the dance duet ends is changed to AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar reaction type. Additionally, after Odette unleashes the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling, should she be in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state while her Dance Double is still on the field, the Dance Double's two dance move types will also deal additional AoE Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.
