## Infobox

Name: Vila Blade Dance
Character: Vesna
Talent Type: Normal Attack
Category: Combat
Description: Normal Attack Performs up to 6 consecutive strikes. Charged Attack Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to trample opponents in front of her. Plunging Attack Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along her path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Vila Blade Dance is Vesna 's Normal Attack .
