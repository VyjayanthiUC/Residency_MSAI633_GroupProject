# Lunar-Charged

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Lunar-Charged | type=mechanic | effective=None -->


## Infobox

Name: Lunar-Charged
Type: Elemental Reactions
Group: Lunar Reactions

Lunar-Charged is the Lunar Reaction triggered by inflicting Electro on a target that is already affected by Hydro or vice versa, replacing Electro-Charged while a character with a corresponding Moonsign Benediction Passive is in the party . After triggering a Lunar-Charged reaction, a thundercloud will appear that continuously deals Electro DMG to nearby enemies that are affected by both Hydro and Electro .


## Damage

Main article: Damage § Lunar Reaction Damage

Once created, thunderclouds will deal Electro DMG to all Wet opponents affected by Electro within 5m [ verification needed ] simultaneously every 2s. If the target has not taken Lunar-Charged DMG in the last 2s and is affected by Wet or Electro , the thundercloud will immediately deal DMG to them as soon as the other aura applied to them.

When a thunderclouds deals DMG to opponents, it will consume 0.4 GU of both the Wet and Electro auras simultaneously. Lunar-Charged shares all other properties of Electro-Charged, including damaging the wards of Fatui Skirmisher - Hydrogunner Legionnaires at an increased rate.

Thunderclouds will try to position themselves between active targets and the triggering party. After being created by a Lunar-Charged reaction, they will last for 6s before disappearing. If another Lunar-Charged reaction is triggered, their duration is reset. Only one thundercloud can exist on the field at a time; if a Lunar-Charged reaction is triggered outside of a thundercloud's range, a new thundercloud will appear at the target location while the previous thundercloud will disappear.


## Tutorial

Elemental Reaction: Lunar-Charged:
- When Electro meets Hydro , and certain other conditions are met, a special Lunar-Charged reaction occurs, and a thundercloud forms. This thundercloud will continuously release bursts of lightning at nearby targets who are affected by both Electro and Hydro , dealing Electro DMG. This DMG can trigger CRIT Hits, and the DMG dealt is based on all characters who have applied elements during the reaction.

## Glossary

Rescue Essentials: Max 150 points. Each Rescue Essentials point Ifa has will increase the Swirl and Electro-Charged DMG dealt by nearby party members by 1.5%, and Lunar-Charged DMG dealt by 0.2%, and Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt by 0.24%. These points will be cleared when his Nightsoul's Blessing state ends.
Lunar Domain: The domain that Columbina summons after unleashing the Elemental Burst Moonlit Melancholy. When current active characters are within the Lunar Domain, Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by all party members will increase.
Gravity Ripple: After unleashing Elemental Skill Eternal Tides, Columbina will summon Gravity Ripple . Gravity Ripple will follow the current active character on the field, while continuously dealing AoE Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents. Additionally, while Gravity Ripple is on the field, and nearby party members trigger Lunar reactions or inflict Lunar Reaction DMG, Columbina will gain the New Moon's Omen effect for 2s, continuously accumulating Gravity. 20 Gravity can be accumulated every 2s. You can have up to 60 Gravity, and it will be removed when the duration of Gravity Ripple ends. Once Gravity is accumulated to its limit, a special Gravity Interference will be triggered. Different effects will be triggered depending on the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina. If the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is: Lunar-Charged: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Electro AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG. Lunar-Bloom: Gravity Interference will fire 5 Moondew Sigils at surrounding opponents, dealing Dendro DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Lunar-Crystallize: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Geo AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Crystallize DMG.
