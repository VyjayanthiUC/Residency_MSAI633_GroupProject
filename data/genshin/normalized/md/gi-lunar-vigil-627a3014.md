Lunar Vigil is Columbina 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Columbina must be in the active party and alive for this passive's effects to work. This applies to both the revival effect and the wildlife -related effects.
- If Columbina is the active character and is incapacitated while this passive is not on cooldown, she will revive herself.
- If a party member is incapacitated while this passive is on cooldown or unavailable, the revival will not activate even after this passive is available again.
- Like with Lauma 's utility passive , certain wildlife will behave differently if Columbina is in the party. This only applies in Nod-Krai, and will not work on the same creatures in other regions . Some creatures will have special interactions. A glowing symbol will briefly appear above them when approached. Blunthorn Rhino - These mine and retrieve nearby Rainbowdrop Crystals . Chic Badger - These dig up Fowl and Raw Meat (amount varies). Puffin - These follow the party around. Rimehorn Deer - These heal the party and fully fill their Energy meters. Wildlife in the " Other " category will not startle when approached. Pale-Furred Wolves will not attack when approached. However, they will do so if the player enters combat or stays near them for more than a few minutes. Unlocks exclusive dialogue with certain animal NPCs , such as Pavlov at the Lucky Spin minigame, the finch beside Richeza at the Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse , and the guard animals near Lyylikki .
