Description: With her impeccable, ladylike poise, Sandrone boards the Tea Party Tactical Assault Hovermech and hovers forward for 6s. During this time, Sandrone can control the travel direction of the craft. Pressing Sprint consumes 10 Stamina and launches her into an elegant high-speed hover mode, in which she will move even faster at the cost of 15 Stamina per second. When Sandrone starts hovering, if there are opponents nearby, she will also summon a Prismatic Resonance Cannon and fire 2 Prism Shots at any opponents who have yet to cool down, dealing Cryo DMG . Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The second Prism Shot will instead deal Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG. When on the Tea Party Tactical Assault Hovermech, Sandrone will also elegantly and quietly repair Fagio, causing Decoding Power to swiftly decrease.

The turning of the gears in various machines, the precise point where the Primordial Sea freezes, the frequency at which the night songs gently drift in the air, the ancient laws of the tides' ebb and flow, and the rising and setting of the moon... All these can be brought under theorems reducible to clean, neat formulas. "Then it does us no harm to admit, as indeed we must, that our gods, our hopes, and the things we love — they can all be defined by reason."

Formule Phenomenale: Differential Analysis is Sandrone 's Elemental Skill .


## Gameplay Notes

- When Prism Shot or Charged Attack Condensed Beam hits at least one enemy , it generates 1 Elemental Particle . This particle generation has a cooldown of 2.5 seconds.
- This particle generation has a cooldown of 2.5 seconds.
- While hovering, the Party Setup menu is accessible even while midair or in high-speed hover mode.
- This skill consumes 10 stamina upon activation and grants Sandrone a brief period of Invincibility Frames .

Passive Effects:

Additionally, for every 10 points of Decoding Power Fagio loses, it gains 1 stack of Refined Tactics for 60s (max 10 stacks). When Sandrone uses the Elemental Burst Formule Phenomenale: Q.E.D. while in the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state, all stacks of Refined Tactics are cleared, causing the Convective Inhibition Ray to deal 100% + (number of stacks cleared) × 10% of its original DMG."

## Glossary

Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: Radiance: Stellar-Conduct and Radiance: Stellar Swirl are both considered Radiance: Stellar Glimmer states. Characters can only be affected by one Radiance state at any one time, and where more than one state can be triggered, Radiance: Stellar-Conduct shall apply first.
Decoding Power: When in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power will continuously increase. When its Decoding Power reaches the maximum, Fagio will be unable to maintain or re-enter Decoding mode.
