"I'll Chase the Shouting Wind Along, Climbing Alone As I Go" is Odette 's Level 3 Constellation .


## Trivia

- The name of her Level 3 Constellation is a reference to the 7 th verse of the sonnet High Flight by World War II fighter pilot and war-poet John Gillespie Magee Jr.
