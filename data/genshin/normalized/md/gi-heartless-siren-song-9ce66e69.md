# Heartless Siren Song

## Infobox

Name: Heartless Siren Song
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: Passive
Category: Passive
Description: As the Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe , Vodyanitsa sings in person in place of playing an instrument.

Heartless Siren Song is Vodyanitsa 's Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- If the player uses a Music Gadget with Vodyanitsa as the active character, she will sing the notes instead of playing the instrument.
