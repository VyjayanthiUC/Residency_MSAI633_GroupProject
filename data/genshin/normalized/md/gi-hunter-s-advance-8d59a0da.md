# Hunter's Advance

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter%27s_Advance | type=talent | effective=2026-08-12 -->


## Infobox

Name: Hunter's Advance
Character: Alyosha
Talent Type: Elemental Burst
Category: Combat
Description: Summons his trusted companion Tugarin to fight alongside him on the field. This also turns an area in front of him into a Fulgurite Hunting Field for a short time. Fulgurite Hunting Field Continuously taunts nearby opponents to incite them to attack. Every 2s, deals an instance of AoE Electro DMG to any opponent that enters the field. Tugarin If there are any opponents nearby, Tugarin will quickly move close to an opponent and maul them every 2s, dealing Electro DMG If an opponent affected by the Hunter's Mark effect is hit, the Hunter's Mark will also be activated. Where there are multiple opponents, Tugarin will attack those affected by the Hunter's Mark effect first.
Energy Cost: 70
Cooldown: 18s

Wait. Keep waiting until the impatience fades. That's when the red-bellied plover shall sweep across the birch grove and present itself before one's arrow.

Hunter's Advance is Alyosha 's Elemental Burst .
