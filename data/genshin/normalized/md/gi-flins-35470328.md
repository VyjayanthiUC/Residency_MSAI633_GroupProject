Kyryll Chudomirovich Flins is a playable Electro character in Genshin Impact .

A Lightkeeper of Nod-Krai , lone guardian of the lighthouse and graveyard on the Final Night Cemetery . Though appearing enigmatic and cold, he is a cultured and courtly gentleman, and one of the last of the Snowland Fae still in Nod-Krai.
