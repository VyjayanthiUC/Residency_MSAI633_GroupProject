# The Last Djeguako Songstress

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last_Djeguako_Songstress | type=talent | effective=2026-09-23 -->


## Infobox

Name: The Last Djeguako Songstress
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: 1st Ascension Passive
Category: Ascension
Description: When the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn is used, any Stellar Vortex on the field will turn into a Wandering Vortex instead. While the Song of Ages Past effect is active, nearby party members' Stellar Swirl reactions will generate Wandering Vortex instead. Generating or detonating a Wandering Vortex will also reduce nearby opponents' Anemo RES by 35% for 6s. Additionally, nearby party members who enter the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state while Song of Ages Past is in effect will have the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state extended by 4s.

The Last Djeguako Songstress is Vodyanitsa 's 1st Ascension Passive .


## Trivia › Etymology

- Djeguako ( Adyghe : джэгуакIо) were bards, musicians and oral historians who performed heroic epics and war songs with stringed instruments for gifts.
