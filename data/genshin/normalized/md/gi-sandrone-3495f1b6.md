Sandrone , also known by her personal name Marionette Guillotin and codename " Marionette ," is a playable Cryo character in Genshin Impact .

She is the Seventh of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers and the leader of the Adventurers' Guild . She interprets all phenomena through the prism of mathematics, and is an elegant lady who is never absent from a tea party.

Sandrone was created by Alain Guillotin in the image of his deceased sister, Mary-Ann Guillotin . Following Alain's death, she kept Pulonia , his final creation, as her constant companion. Since Pulonia's death, she has been accompanied by a new robotic companion named Fagio ( Facilitatrice Automatisee Generaliste Interactive et Omnitache ).

Upon obtaining Sandrone, the World Quest A Lady's Invitation becomes available, and completing it rewards Sandrone's Portable Tea Table .

## Glossary

Decoding: Fagio will switch to this mode when using a Charged Attack. When in this mode, Fagio will engage any opponents ahead with a sweeping fire attack, as well as fire condensed beams at intervals, dealing AoE Cryo DMG . Radiance: Stellar Glimmer : The condensed beams will instead deal AoE Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer DMG.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: Radiance: Stellar-Conduct and Radiance: Stellar Swirl are both considered Radiance: Stellar Glimmer states. Characters can only be affected by one Radiance state at any one time, and where more than one state can be triggered, Radiance: Stellar-Conduct shall apply first.
Decoding Power: When in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power will continuously increase. When its Decoding Power reaches the maximum, Fagio will be unable to maintain or re-enter Decoding mode.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
Polestar Field: When Stellar-Conduct is triggered, the surounding area will transform into a Polestar Field for a short period of time, and when inside a Polestar Field, certain characters will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state, when grants various skill buffs.
