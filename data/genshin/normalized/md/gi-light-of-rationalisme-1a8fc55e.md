# Light of Rationalisme

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Light_of_Rationalisme | type=talent | effective=2026-07-01 -->


## Infobox

Name: Light of Rationalisme
Character: Sandrone
Talent Type: Stellar Jubilee Passive
Category: Passive
Description: Sandrone will enter the Radiance: Stellar Conduct state when she is inside a Polestar Field , or the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s after a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction. When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of the aforementioned reaction is also increased by 0.7% for every 100 points of Sandrone's ATK. A maximum increase of 14% can be obtained in this way.
utility1: Utility Elemental Reaction Enhancement Elemental Reaction Base DMG Bonus Stellar-Conduct Enabler and Base DMG Bonus Stellar Swirl Enabler and Base DMG Bonus Stellar Glimmer Reaction Enabling

Light of Rationalisme is Sandrone 's Stellar Jubilee Passive .
