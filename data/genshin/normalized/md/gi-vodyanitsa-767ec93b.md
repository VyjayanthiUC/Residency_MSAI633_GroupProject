Vodyanitsa ( Russian : Водяница Vodyanitsa ) is a playable Hydro character in Genshin Impact .

A Water Imp from Snezhnaya and the Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe , she is renowned for her mesmerizing singing voice, which is said to conceal an otherworldly allure capable of bewitching those who hear it.

## Glossary

Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn: Reveals her true Water Imp form, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and gaining theSong of Ages Past effect: While this effect is active, Vodyanitsa summons the ancientHorn of Spring's Call, periodically moving closer to an opponent in a swift movement to attack them, dealing Hydro DMG . The above two types of attacks will also decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of opponents hit. Additionally, while theSong of Ages Past effect is active, the party's active character gains increased resistance to interruption and periodically regenerates HP based on Vodyanitsa's Max HP.
Lead Vocal: When the party's currently active character deals Hydro DMG or Cryo DMG with a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, Plunging Attack, Elemental Skill, or Elemental Burst, 1 stack of "Lead Vocal" is consumed. This in turn increases the DMG dealt. However, if a Wandering Vortex is present on the field or during the 5s following a Wandering Vortex explosion, this effect will only trigger when the character deals Stellar Swirl reaction DMG. Every 1000 Max HP Vodyanitsa has in excess of 40000 will increase the Stellar Swirl reaction DMG from the above effect by 260, as well as Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG dealt by 140. Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt can be increased by a maximum of 6500 while Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG can be increased by a maximum of 3500 in this way.
Chorus: Triggered by off-field party members or other nearby party members (and not your currently active party member). Effects are the same as those of "Lead Vocal."
Koda: Sink With Thee: Leads a song of the Water Imps which lures its listeners to their doom. This deals AoE Hydro DMG to the opponent. When in the Song of Ages Past state, DMG dealt is further increased.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
