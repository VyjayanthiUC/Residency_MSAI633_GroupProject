## Infobox

Name: Witchseeker's Vow
Character: Prune
Talent Type: Witch's Eve Rite Passive
Category: Additional
Description: After completing Witch's Homework : Of What Is Contested , Prune becomes a Hexerei character. When the party includes at least 2 Hexerei characters, you gain the Hexerei: Secret Rite effect, which enhances Hexerei characters. Hexerei: Secret Rite After a Hexerei character in the party affected by the Tolling Rally effect triggers an Elemental Reaction on an opponent, Prune's ATK is increased by 60% for 5s. If the Elemental Reaction triggered is a Swirl Reaction, the triggering character's ATK will additionally be increased by 30% for 5s.
utility1: Utility ATK Increase

Witchseeker's Vow is Prune 's Witch's Eve Rite Passive .
