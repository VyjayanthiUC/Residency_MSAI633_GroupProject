Radiance Over Blossoms and Peaks is Columbina 's Level 1 Constellation .


## Gameplay Notes

- As the appropriate Lunar reaction type of the instant Gravity Interference effect is not guided by the gradual accumulation of Gravity through normal combat, it is instead determined directly based on the Elemental Types of characters in the party. When there is a majority of Electro , Dendro or Geo characters (with a fixed tie-breaker priority in this same order), the instant Gravity Interference will behave as if that respective element's Lunar reaction type had accumulated the most Gravity.

## Glossary

Gravity Ripple: After unleashing Elemental Skill Eternal Tides, Columbina will summon Gravity Ripple. Gravity Ripple will follow the current active character on the field, while continuously dealing AoE Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents. Additionally, while Gravity Ripple is on the field, and nearby party members trigger Lunar reactions or inflict Lunar Reaction DMG, Columbina will gain the New Moon's Omen effect for 2s, continuously accumulating Gravity. 20 Gravity can be accumulated every 2s. You can have up to 60 Gravity, and it will be removed when the duration of Gravity Ripple ends. Once Gravity is accumulated to its limit, a special Gravity Interference will be triggered. Different effects will be triggered depending on the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina. If the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is: Lunar-Charged: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Electro AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG. Lunar-Bloom: Gravity Interference will fire 5 Moondew Sigils at surrounding opponents, dealing Dendro DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Lunar-Crystallize: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Geo AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Crystallize DMG.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
