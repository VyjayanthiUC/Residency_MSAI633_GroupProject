## Adjustments & Optimizations

Miliastra Wonderland:

- A new "Public Channel" has been added to Miliastra Wonderland , and Travelers can share Cosmetics , recruit teammates, and recruit in the lobby via the Public Channel.
- In Miliastra Wonderland, you can now initiate private chats with strangers. Press the corresponding Traveler's avatar, then press the " Chat " button to initiate.
- In Miliastra Wonderland, the maximum capacity of the Public Lobby has been adjusted from 20 to 40.
- Optimizes the filtering function in the "Search Wonderlands" interface: The "Type" option is now displayed directly on the interface, and new options have been added to the filters.
- Optimizes the material effects of the Cosmetic " Galaxy Wanderer Robe " (Top) while in motion.
- Adds an audio input setting option to Settings > Audio on mobile devices.

Serenitea Pot:

- In the Editing Screen, when the area's load reaches a certain threshold, an indicator will appear beneath the furnishing icon, helping you quickly determine if that furnishing exceeds the load.
- After reaching Trust Rank 10, the crafting time for furnishings will be reduced by 4 hours, and the upper limit of the furnishing creation queue will be increased from 10 to 15.
- " Realm Music " now displays tracks that have not yet been obtained and indicates the regions where their Radiant Spincrystals can be found. In addition, some tracks can now be previewed in this interface.
- Optimizes the display of companions who prefer the selected Gift Set in the Editing Screen.

System:

- Optimizes the "Auto Add" logic in the Character Level Up and Weapon / Artifact Enhance interfaces: When auto adding materials, the system will now take current Mora into account, preventing cases where there isn't enough Mora after materials are added.
- Reduces the frequency of the overflow EXP hint pop-up in the Character > Level Up screen: When the overflow EXP from leveling up is less than one " Adventurer's Experience " (i.e., 5000 EXP), the pop-up hint will no longer appear.
- In the " Artifact " "Lock Assistance" feature, the number of "Recommended Set Plan" for the same set will be increased, and the number of custom configurations that can be saved has been adjusted from 2 to 3.

Audio:

- Optimizes the sound performance of the gadget " Harmonic Keys ."
- Optimizes the sound effects in some areas of Nod-Krai , as well as the music performance in some World Quests .
- Optimizes the wording of some Japanese voice-over lines for the character " Varka " in the character's Profile > Voice-Over interface.
- Supplements English voice-over lines for updated content between Version 5.2 and Version 5.7 . (The English voice-over lines for these versions are now complete with the exception of five remaining lines that will be added in future updates.)

Other:

- Optimizes the text descriptions for certain Brilliant Blessings in Imaginarium Theater .
- Rainbowdrop Crystals can now be pulled by certain skills or effects.

Genius Invokation TCG Balance Adjustment:

- Adjusts the effect of Event Card " Void Rift " for the Character Card " Skirk ": Playing this card will be considered a "Combat Action" and can only be played if you have a card in your Hand that currently costs 3 Elemental Dice.
- Increases the starting Max HP of Barbara 's Character Card from 10 to 12.
- Adjusts the DMG of Eula 's Summon "Lightfall Sword": Physical DMG increased from 3 to 4.
- Adjusts the DMG of Freminet 's state "Pers Timer": "...deals an additional 2 Physical DMG" has been increased to "...deals an additional 3 Physical DMG."
- Adjusted the effect of Arataki Itto 's Summon "Ushi": It now deals 1 Geo DMG at the end phase and also grants Arataki Itto Superlative Superstrength.
- Adjusts the effect of the Event Card " Mystique Soup: Fury ": The effect "...the next DMG dealt by the target character +2." has been adjusted to "The next DMG dealt by the target character +1. (Usage(s): 2)"
- Adjusts the effect of the Support Card " Tonatiuh ": The effect "...draw 1 card." has been adjusted to "...draw 2 cards."
