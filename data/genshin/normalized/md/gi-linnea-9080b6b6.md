Linnea is a playable Geo character in Genshin Impact .

Raised among humans as one of the last Snowland Fae in Nod-Krai, as well as one of the last Augury Birds in existence, Linnea currently works as an advisor to the Adventurers' Guild and as a naturalist.

Upon obtaining Linnea, the Gadget Linnea's Naturalist Archive may be obtained through the character menu, which includes the badge Unadorned and companion Companion: Exclusive .

## Glossary

Lumi: Strike Forms: Lumi has three different Strike Forms. Super Power Form Lumi will use Pound-Pound Pummeler to attack nearby opponents continuously. Every attack will deal 2 instances of AoE Geo DMG . If there are Moondrifts nearby, Lumi will alternate between Pound-Pound Pummeler and Heavy Overdrive Hammer to continuously attack nearby opponents . Heavy Overdrive Hammer will deal AoE Geo DMG which will be considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG. Ultimate Power Form Lumi will use the ultimate Million Ton Crush, dealing an especially powerful instance of AoE Geo DMG that is considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG, switching to Standard Power Form. Standard Power Form It might not be as flashy as the other forms, but it still packs a serious punch. Lumi will use Pound-Pound Pummeler at longer intervals to attack nearby opponents continuously. Every attack will deal 2 instances of AoE Geo DMG .
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
