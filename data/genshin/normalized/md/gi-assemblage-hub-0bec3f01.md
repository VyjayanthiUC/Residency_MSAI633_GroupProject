## Infobox

Name: Assemblage Hub
Character: Ineffa
Talent Type: Moonsign Benediction Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When a party member triggers the Electro-Charged reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Charged reaction, with every 100 ATK that Ineffa has increasing Lunar-Charged's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%. Additionally, when Ineffa is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.
scale_att1: Scaling ATK
utility1: Utility Elemental Reaction Enhancement Elemental Reaction Base DMG Bonus Lunar-Charged Enabler and Base DMG Bonus Lunar Reaction Enabling

Assemblage Hub is Ineffa 's Moonsign Benediction Passive .
