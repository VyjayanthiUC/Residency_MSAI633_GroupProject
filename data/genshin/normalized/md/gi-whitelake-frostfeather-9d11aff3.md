The following two scripts are from the Palestar Songbook, which precedes the present day by around 300 years. Their lines, tone, rhythm, and even sentences are almost identical, and it is impossible to tell which was written first. ## Trivia

- The first passages of both scripts, where Tatyana and Vaselisa speak to their respective fathers, reference the poem Erlkönig , in which the titular king of fairies attempts to lure a boy away from his father.
- The line "this stage, though huge, is but a scene in a play" may be a reference to the line " All the world's a stage " from As You Like It by William Shakespeare.
