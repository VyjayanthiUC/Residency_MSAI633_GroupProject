A longsword that once shone with the light of the very stars. In the voyager's hand, it becomes a brand-new light that has nothing to do with any past era.

Exaiphanes Blade is a 5-Star Sword obtained during Archon Quest Chapter VII : Act I - Everwinter Without Mercy . It is powered up by each Statue of The Seven unlocked in Snezhnaya .


## Ascensions and Stats

The Exaiphanes Blade requires no Weapon Enhancement Materials , Weapon Ascension Materials , or Mora to level up, ascend, or refine.

After completing Step 23 of Archon Quest Great Deeds on the Tundra in Chapter VII : Act I - Everwinter Without Mercy , the player can improve the Exaiphanes Blade by applying Cryo to a Statue of The Seven in Snezhnaya , interacting with it, and selecting Enhance Exaiphanes Blade . This will increase its Ascension Phase, Refinement Rank, affixes, and/or passive effects. Unlike Traveler's resonance , the party does not need to have the Traveler nor the sword equipped to upgrade it.

Each Statue of The Seven can only be used to upgrade the Exaiphanes Blade by one stage. If this feature is available at a given Statue, the following message will appear under its map description: Can enhance the Exaiphanes Blade at this Statue of The Seven . If it is not available at a given Statue, the dialogue option will change to View Exaiphanes Blade.
