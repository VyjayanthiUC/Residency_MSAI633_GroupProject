# Lunar-Bloom

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Lunar-Bloom | type=mechanic | effective=2025-09-10 -->


## Infobox

Name: Lunar-Bloom
Type: Elemental Reactions
Group: Lunar Reactions

Lunar-Bloom is the Lunar Reaction triggered by inflicting Dendro on a target that is already affected by Hydro or vice versa, replacing Bloom while a character with a corresponding Moonsign Benediction Passive is in the party . When Lunar-Bloom is triggered, it will grant the party a Verdant Dew in addition to spawning a Dendro Core or Bountiful Core . Verdant Dew can be consumed by certain Talents to deal Lunar-Bloom DMG.


## Damage

Main article: Damage § Lunar Reaction Damage

Lunar-Bloom does no DMG on its own. Currently, the only way to deal Lunar-Bloom DMG is through character Talents and Constellations.


## Damage › Verdant Dew

When Lunar-Bloom is triggered, the player will gain 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s for 2.5s. The player can have up to 3 Verdant Dew at a time, and when Lunar-Bloom is triggered again while the player is gaining Verdant Dew, the duration will be reset. Verdant Dew lasts indefinitely and persists through party changes, teleportation between maps and Domains , daily resets, and game sessions.


## Tutorial

Elemental Reaction: Lunar-Bloom:
- When Dendro meets Hydro , and certain other conditions are met, a special Lunar-Bloom reaction occurs. In addition to the standard Bloom reaction effects, this also provides the party with valuable "Verdant Dew." Certain characters can consume "Verdant Dew" or utilize their Talents to deal Lunar-Bloom DMG to enemies. This DMG can trigger CRIT Hits.

Verdant Dew:
- Within 2.5s after party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, your party will receive 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s. Your party can have up to 3 Verdant Dew. If you trigger another Lunar-Bloom reaction during this time, the duration of this effect will be reset. Certain characters can consume Verdant Dew to deal Lunar-Bloom DMG to enemies.

## Glossary

Pale Hymn: A hymn passed down by generations of Moonchanters that increases the DMG dealt by Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, and Lunar-Bloom reactions. Lauma can gain Pale Hymn stacks in the following ways: When using her Elemental Burst Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon. When consuming Moon Song stacks due to using her Elemental Burst Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon. When the Frostgrove Sanctuary triggers the effect of her Constellation "I Offer Blood and Tears to the Moonlight". When party members deal Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Lunar-Bloom DMG, 1 stack of Pale Hymn will be consumed and the DMG dealt will be increased based on Lauma's Elemental Mastery. If this DMG hits multiple opponents at once, then multiple stacks of Pale Hymn will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit. The duration for each stack of Pale Hymn is counted independently.
Moon Song: A melody used to weave together hymns. When an Elemental Burst is used, or for a period of time after an Elemental Burst has been used, it can be converted into Pale Hymn stacks that increase the DMG from Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, and Lunar-Bloom reactions. When Lauma consumes Verdant Dew to perform the Hold version of her Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko, each Verdant Dew consumed will provide Lauma with 1 stack of Moon Song.
Lunar Domain: The domain that Columbina summons after unleashing the Elemental Burst Moonlit Melancholy. When current active characters are within the Lunar Domain, Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by all party members will increase.
Gravity Ripple: After unleashing Elemental Skill Eternal Tides, Columbina will summon Gravity Ripple . Gravity Ripple will follow the current active character on the field, while continuously dealing AoE Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents. Additionally, while Gravity Ripple is on the field, and nearby party members trigger Lunar reactions or inflict Lunar Reaction DMG, Columbina will gain the New Moon's Omen effect for 2s, continuously accumulating Gravity. 20 Gravity can be accumulated every 2s. You can have up to 60 Gravity, and it will be removed when the duration of Gravity Ripple ends. Once Gravity is accumulated to its limit, a special Gravity Interference will be triggered. Different effects will be triggered depending on the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina. If the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is: Lunar-Charged: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Electro AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG. Lunar-Bloom: Gravity Interference will fire 5 Moondew Sigils at surrounding opponents, dealing Dendro DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Lunar-Crystallize: Gravity Interference will deal a single instance of Geo AoE DMG . This DMG is considered Lunar-Crystallize DMG.
Verdant Dew: When party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party will receive such a resource. Your party will receive 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5s for 2.5s after triggering a Lunar-Bloom reaction. Your party can have up to 3 Verdant Dew. If you trigger another Lunar-Bloom reaction during this time, the duration of this effect will be reset.
Frostgrove Sanctuary: A magical array that protects the Moonchanters and can be summoned by using the Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko. The Frostgrove Sanctuary will follow the active character, dealing a single instance of AoE Dendro DMG to opponents within the array every 2s. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
Shadow Dance: After using the Elemental Skill Senet Strategy: Dance of a Thousand Nights, Nefer will enter this state. While in the Shadow Dance state, if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Nefer's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Phantasm Performance. Nefer will exit the Shadow Dance state when she unleashes 3 Phantasm Performance(s) or the state's duration ends.
Phantasm Performance: When in the Shadow Dance state caused by her Elemental Skill Senet Strategy: Dance of a Thousand Nights, if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Nefer's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Phantasm Performance, which will not consume Stamina. Nefer summons shades of herself to perform Coordinated Attacks against enemies. Nefer and the shades will deal 2 and 3 stages of AoE Dendro DMG respectively. DMG dealt by the shades is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. 1 Verdant Dew will be consumed the first time shades are summoned after every Phantasm Performance.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
