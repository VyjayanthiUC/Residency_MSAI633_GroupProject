# Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Rechitativ%3A_Sonorous_Dawn | type=talent | effective=2026-09-23 -->


## Infobox

Name: Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: Elemental Skill
Category: Combat
Description: Reveals her true Water Imp form, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and gaining the Song of Ages Past effect: While this effect is active, Vodyanitsa summons the ancient Horn of Spring's Call, periodically moving closer to an opponent in a swift movement to attack them, dealing Hydro DMG . The above two types of attacks will also decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of opponents hit. Additionally, while the Song of Ages Past effect is active, the party's active character gains increased resistance to interruption and periodically regenerates HP based on Vodyanitsa's Max HP.
Cooldown: 16s

"I shall not lament, nor shall I call out or weep. All things shall pass, like mist that softly drifts across the boughs of the white apple trees." "And yet, and yet — for all living things upon the earth, I wish you everlasting beauty and happiness."

Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn is Vodyanitsa 's Elemental Skill .
