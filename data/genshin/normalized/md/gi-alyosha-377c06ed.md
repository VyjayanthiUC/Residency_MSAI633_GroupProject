Alyosha ( Russian : Алёша Alyosha ) is a playable Electro character in Genshin Impact .

He is a renowned hunter hailing from the Snezhnayan tundra, operating alongside his companion Tugarin .

## Glossary

Hunter's Mark: A special effect Alyosha can apply on the opponent. When this effect is applied to an opponent who is already under the Hunter's Mark effect, it will activate Hunter's Mark instead, which removes said mark and grants all nearby party members the Hunter's Precision effect, which increases their ATK when they are on the field.
Tugarin: Alyosha's trusted hunting hound and companion, which can be summoned to the field using the Elemental Burst Hunter's Advance. Periodically moves close to an opponent to maul them, and activate any existing Hunter's Marks.
