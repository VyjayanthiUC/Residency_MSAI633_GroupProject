Tartaglia , also known by his codename " Childe ," is a playable Hydro character in Genshin Impact .

He is the Eleventh of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers . Wherever he goes, danger follows, and Childe is always eager for a challenge, making him extremely dangerous despite being the youngest member.

That is, suppose there are n {\displaystyle n} enemies each affected by Riptide grouped close together. Each Riptide Slash will do local AoE damage hitting surrounding enemies. So there will be n {\displaystyle n} Riptide Slashes, each hitting n − 1 {\displaystyle n-1} enemies. ## Gameplay Info › Talents › Character Description

Tartaglia can switch between Melee and Ranged Stances at will. In his Ranged Stance , Aimed Shots will apply Riptide upon hitting opponents. Opponents affected by Riptide take bonus damage from melee attacks. Elemental Burst triggered in Ranged Stance will inflict Riptide on all opponents hit by the AoE. Tartaglia's Elemental Skill allows him to enter his Melee Stance , causing his normal attacks to deal Hydro DMG . An Elemental Burst activated while in Melee Stance will trigger an explosion on all opponents affected by Riptide within the AoE .
