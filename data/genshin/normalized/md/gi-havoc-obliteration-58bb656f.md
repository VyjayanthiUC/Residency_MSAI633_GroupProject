Rushing waters are rarely seen in his snowy homeland . Perhaps his ability to command water is due to his understanding of just how precious it is.

Havoc: Obliteration is Tartaglia 's Elemental Burst .


## Gameplay Notes

- The Melee Stance version of this Elemental Burst drains energy later than most others.
- For more information on Riptide, see Cutting Torrent .
- If an enemy would die in one hit from the Range Stance version, Riptide Burst will still trigger, even if the enemy did not have Riptide applied to them before using the Elemental Burst.
- If an enemy with Riptide dies from the first hit of the Melee Stance version, both Riptide Blast and Riptide Burst will trigger.
- If an enemy dies to the Riptide Blast effect of this Elemental Burst, it will not trigger Riptide Burst from Cutting Torrent , as the Riptide status is consumed first to apply the damage of Riptide Blast.
- After the Melee Stance Elemental Burst hits an enemy with Riptide, there is a 0.8s delay before Riptide Blast occurs and the removal of Riptide. If Riptide naturally expires during this delay, Riptide Blast will still trigger.

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 5, Havoc: Formless Blade : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
- Constellation Level 6, Havoc: Annihilation : "When Havoc: Obliteration is cast in Melee Stance, the CD of Foul Legacy: Raging Tide is reset. This effect will only take place once Tartaglia returns to his Ranged Stance."
