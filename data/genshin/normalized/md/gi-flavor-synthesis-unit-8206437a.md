## Infobox

Name: Flavor Synthesis Unit
Character: Ineffa
Talent Type: Utility Passive
Category: Passive
Description: When Ineffa uses food , there is a 30% chance of gaining a seasoning ingredient. Additionally, in Nod-Krai , it seems you'll be able to change Birgitta 's appearance through certain means...

Flavor Synthesis Unit is Ineffa 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Ineffa must be in the active party for Flavor Synthesis Unit to take effect, but she does not need to be alive. The exception is for Recovery Dishes , which must be consumed by Ineffa to produce seasoning ingredients.
- "Seasoning" ingredients that can be produced by this passive include: Cream Pepper Salt Spice Sugar
- Cream
- Pepper
- Salt
- Spice
- Sugar
- The seasoning ingredients do not correspond to the food items consumed.
- Seasoning ingredients can be produced from all items in the Food tab, including potions , items like Apples , and all Other Dishes .
- Additionally, Birgitta 's appearance can be altered by purchasing Ineffa's Toolbox from the Workshop's Trove .
