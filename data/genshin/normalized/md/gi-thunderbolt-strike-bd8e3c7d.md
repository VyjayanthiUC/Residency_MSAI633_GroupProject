# Thunderbolt Strike

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Thunderbolt_Strike | type=talent | effective=2026-08-12 -->


## Infobox

Name: Thunderbolt Strike
Character: Alyosha
Talent Type: Elemental Skill
Category: Combat
Description: Follows his hunter's instincts. Tap or hold to produce different effects as well as apply the Hunter's Mark effect to the hit opponents. Tapping Fires at opponents in front of him, dealing AoE Electro DMG . Holding Enters aiming mode, then targets opponents within a certain area in front of him. Alyosha's Interruption RES is enhanced when in this mode. At the end of the button hold, he deals Electro DMG to the targeted opponents.
Cooldown: 15s

The reckless hunter once reached for the spear's edge, and it was only after they survived a lesson paid in blood that they came to understand the harsh truths of survival on the frozen plains.

Thunderbolt Strike is Alyosha 's Elemental Skill .


## Gameplay Notes

- When this skill hits at least one enemy , it generates 5 Elemental Particles .

## Glossary

Hunter's Mark: A special effect Alyosha can apply on the opponent. When this effect is applied to an opponent who is already under the Hunter's Mark effect, it will activate Hunter's Mark instead, which removes said mark and grants all nearby party members the Hunter's Precision effect, which increases their ATK when they are on the field.
