A Light in the Dark is Flins 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Flins must be in the active party for A Light in the Dark to take effect, but he does not need to be alive.
- Only one local specialty will be shown on the mini-map at a time.
- The following 2 Talents are identical to this talent: Miniaturized Detection Sensor ( Aino ) Prayers for the Forest ( Lauma )
- Miniaturized Detection Sensor ( Aino )
- Prayers for the Forest ( Lauma )
- Additionally, having Flins on-field will allow the player to understand the speech of Wilderness Exiles and Wilderness Hunters .
