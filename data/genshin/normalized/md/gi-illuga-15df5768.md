Illuga is a playable Geo character in Genshin Impact .

Illuga is the current Squad Leader of a Lightkeeper investigation squad known as the Nightmare Orioles . His past experiences dealing with the Wild Hunt has driven him to care deeply for every squadmate under his command and ensure they never face the same tragedies he once endured.
