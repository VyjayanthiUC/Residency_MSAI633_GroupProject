# Echoes Over the Frozen Lake

<!-- https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Echoes_Over_the_Frozen_Lake | type=talent | effective=2026-09-23 -->


## Infobox

Name: Echoes Over the Frozen Lake
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: Passive
Category: Passive
Description: Holding the Elemental Skill outside of combat will cause Vodyanitsa to embody her true Water Imp form and swim for up to 10s at the cost of Stamina. Naturally, as a Water Imp, she can also move across the water's surface. Vodyanitsa will exit her Water Imp form when the Elemental Skill is used again, when the duration ends, or when all Stamina is depleted. Additionally, while Vodyanitsa can swim faster, her Stamina consumption rate is also increased.

Echoes Over the Frozen Lake is Vodyanitsa 's Passive .
