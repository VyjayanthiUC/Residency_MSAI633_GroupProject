## Infobox

Name: Snow Swan Variation
Character: Odette
Talent Type: Normal Attack
Category: Combat
Description: Normal Attack Unleashes a sword attack of up to 5 consecutive strikes. Charged Attack Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a dazzling slash on opponents in front of her. Plunging Attack Attacks opponents in her path while plunging from mid-air, then deals AoE DMG upon landing.

Snow Swan Variation is Odette 's Normal Attack .
