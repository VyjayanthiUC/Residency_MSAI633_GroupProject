Conspiracy of the Golden Vault is Nefer 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- This passive grants Nefer access to the Intel Hunt feature, which is only available to members of the Curatorium of Secrets . When Nefer is near certain NPCs , her Elemental Skill will be replaced with a special intel-gathering skill. Using it on these NPCs will reveal additional information about them and potentially unlock exclusive dialogue and/or quests . For each NPC, gathering intel from them for the first time will reward Recorded Intel ×1 . Recorded Intel can be exchanged with Katheryne to unlock Intel Quests . The intel-gathering skill has a fixed cooldown of 8.5 seconds, but uses Nefer's standard Elemental Skill animation. Curatorium members can gather intel from a combined total of 6 new NPCs per Weekly Reset . Attempting to gather more intel with Nefer will trigger the following dialogue: Media:vo mwgwq002 1 nefer 09.ogg (Alright, time for a break. That's enough intel for now.)
