## Infobox

Name: Psyshkhwe Arietta
Character: Vodyanitsa
Talent Type: Normal Attack
Category: Combat
Description: Normal Attack Sings an ancient song of the Water Imps, unleashing up to 4 strikes. This deals Hydro DMG to the opponent. Charged Attack Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to hurl a water ball forward, dealing Hydro DMG . Plunging Attack Plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents along the path. Deals AoE Hydro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Psyshkhwe Arietta is Vodyanitsa 's Normal Attack.

## Trivia › Etymology

- Psyshkhwe ( Adyghe : Псышхъуэ ) is Adyghe , псы [psə] " water " + шхъуэ [ʃχwə](or щхъуэ in Kabardian) " grey–blue–green " .The literal internal form is "water's grey‐blue" or "the blue‐green hue of water" .
- This interpretation corresponds to its Chinese rendering .
