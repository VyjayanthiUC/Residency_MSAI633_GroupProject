## Infobox

Name: Ancient Rite: Arcane Light
Character: Flins
Talent Type: Elemental Skill
Category: Combat
Description: Flins summons forth the ancient power concealed within his lamp and switches to his Manifest Flame form. This form has the following characteristics: Flins's Normal and Charged Attacks deal Electro DMG that cannot be overridden by other Elemental infusions, and he is unable to perform Plunging Attacks. His interruption resistance is increased. His Elemental Skill Ancient Rite: Arcane Light is replaced with the special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm. Northland Spearstorm Flins summons a flurry of spears in front, dealing AoE Electro DMG and causing his Elemental Burst Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night to be replaced with the special Elemental Burst Thunderous Symphony for the next 6s. The base CD of Northland Spearstorm is 6s and will not be affected by other effects.
scale_att1: Scaling ATK
utility1: Utility Conversion Interruption Resistance Plunging Attack Disabled Requires Staying on Field Hitlag Extendable
Cooldown: 16s

"We shall part the snow-curtain of the sky, and blast the winter winds across the wasteland."

Ancient Rite: Arcane Light is Flins 's Elemental Skill .


## Gameplay Notes

- When a converted Normal Attack hits at least one enemy , it generates 1 Elemental Particle . This particle generation has a cooldown of 2 seconds.
- This particle generation has a cooldown of 2 seconds.
- This skill's activation produces a damageless 0U Electro attack around Flins.

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 5, Exile's Shadow : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
