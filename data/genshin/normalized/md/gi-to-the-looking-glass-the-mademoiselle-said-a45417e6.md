To the Looking-Glass the Mademoiselle Said is a Character Event Wish with the 5 Star Character "Mirrored Analysis" Sandrone ( Cryo ) as the promoted character with an increased chance to be obtained.

## Trivia

- The name of this Wish Series is likely a reference to the song To the Looking-Glass World it was Alice that Said... that appears in Through the Looking-Glass by Lewis Caroll .
- The first occurrence of this Wish Series ran alongside an Epitome Invocation Wish featuring A Teaspoon of Transcendence , a weapon tailored to Sandrone's gameplay mechanics.
