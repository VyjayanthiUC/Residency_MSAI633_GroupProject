The Hearth's Ashen Shadow is a Character Event Wish with the 5 Star Character "Dire Balemoon" Arlecchino ( Pyro ) as the promoted character with an increased chance to be obtained.

## Trivia

- All occurrences of this Wish Series have run alongside an Epitome Invocation Wish featuring Crimson Moon's Semblance , a weapon that both is tailored to Arlecchino's gameplay mechanics and can transform into a scythe only when wielded by Arlecchino.
