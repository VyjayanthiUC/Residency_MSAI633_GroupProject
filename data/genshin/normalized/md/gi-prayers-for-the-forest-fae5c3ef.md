Prayers for the Forest is Lauma 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Lauma must be in the active party for Prayers for the Forest to take effect. She does not need to be alive to locate local specialties, but does need to be alive to enable the passive's wildlife-related effects.
- She does not need to be alive to locate local specialties, but does need to be alive to enable the passive's wildlife-related effects.
- Only one local specialty will be shown on the mini-map at a time.
- Certain wildlife will behave differently if Lauma is in the party. This only applies in Nod-Krai, and will not work on the same creatures in other regions . Some creatures will have special interactions. A glowing symbol will briefly appear above them when approached. Blunthorn Rhino - These mine and retrieve nearby Rainbowdrop Crystals . Chic Badger - These dig up Fowl and Raw Meat (amount varies). Puffin - These follow the party around. Rimehorn Deer - These heal the party and fully fill their Energy meters. Wildlife in the " Other " category will not startle when approached. Pale-Furred Wolves will not attack when approached. However, they will do so if the player enters combat or stays near them for more than a few minutes. Unlocks exclusive dialogue with certain animal NPCs , such as Pavlov at the Lucky Spin minigame, the finch beside Richeza at the Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse , and the guard animals near Lyylikki .
