## Infobox

Name: Moonlit Melancholy
Character: Columbina
Talent Type: Elemental Burst
Category: Combat
Description: Hills and tides unite under the silver light of a pristine new moon. Temporarily transforms the surrounding terrain into Lunar Domain , dealing AoE Hydro DMG .
scale_att1: Scaling Max HP
utility1: Utility Lunar-Charged DMG Bonus Lunar-Bloom DMG Bonus Lunar-Crystallize DMG Bonus
Energy Cost: 60
Cooldown: 15s

In ancient times, people called the three messengers connecting the material world and the universe "Iynx," which means bird-like gods.

Moonlit Melancholy is Columbina 's Elemental Burst .


## Gameplay Notes

Constellation Effects:

- Constellation Level 5, Silence Tending One Lone Song : Increases this talent's level by 3, up to a maximum of 15.
- Constellation Level 6, Through Darkness Led by Moonlight : "For 8s after characters in the Lunar Domain trigger a Lunar reaction, based on the elements involved in the reaction, the CRIT DMG of the corresponding Elemental DMG done by all party members is increased by 80%. The CRIT DMG-increasing effects for the same Elemental Type do not stack. All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 7%."

## Glossary

Lunar Domain: The domain that Columbina summons after unleashing the Elemental Burst Moonlit Melancholy. When current active characters are within the Lunar Domain, Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by all party members will increase.
Elevation: Special DMG Boost effect. It is calculated independently of other DMG Boost effects.
