Echo of Winter Daydreams is Odette 's Utility Passive .


## Gameplay Notes

- Odette must be in the active party for Echo of Winter Daydreams to take effect, but she does not need to be alive.
- Only one local specialty will be shown on the mini-map at a time.
-
- The following Talent is identical to this talent: Treetop Watch ( Alyosha )
- Treetop Watch ( Alyosha )
- When Odette is in her dancing idle animation, her Elemental Skill is replaced by a button that can be tapped repeatedly to extend her dance. Tapping her Elemental Skill will add additional pirouettes and fouettés to her performance, extending her idle animation to a maximum of ~30s. While in Snezhnaya, during Odette's dancing idle animation, a variation of a section of Odette's Character Trailer theme plays.
- Tapping her Elemental Skill will add additional pirouettes and fouettés to her performance, extending her idle animation to a maximum of ~30s.
- While in Snezhnaya, during Odette's dancing idle animation, a variation of a section of Odette's Character Trailer theme plays.


## Trivia

- The name of her Utility Passive is a reference to Winter Daydreams (Symphony No. 1 in G Minor) by Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky .
