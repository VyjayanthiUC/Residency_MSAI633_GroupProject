# Genshin Impact Wiki Assistant

Answers questions about Genshin Impact's **Nod-Krai** and **Snezhnaya** content using only pages from the
Genshin Impact Fandom wiki snapshot. Every factual claim cites its source page; when the snapshot doesn't
cover a question, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

- Content is a snapshot — check the date shown with each answer, and the live wiki for anything time-sensitive.
- Questions about payments, refunds, or accounts are referred to official HoYoverse support.
